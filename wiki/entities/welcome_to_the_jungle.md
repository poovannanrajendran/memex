## Overview
Previously known as Otta, Welcome to the Jungle is a job platform that aims to provide a better user experience for job seekers compared to traditional platforms like LinkedIn. It focuses on helping users get hired effectively.

## Role in this knowledge base
A highly-rated job portal praised for its user-centric design and focus on facilitating hiring.
