## Overview
A town in Kent, located west of Tonbridge, known for its affluent status and proximity to London. It is a popular commuter town with high property prices.

## Role in this knowledge base
Presented as a benchmark for commuting but significantly more expensive than Tonbridge.

## Key facts
- Sevenoaks is approximately 12 minutes by train from London.
