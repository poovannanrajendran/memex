## Overview
Omni Route is a plugin that expands Claude Code's access to various AI models, connecting it to over 300 free AI providers to ensure continuous usage and access to substantial free tokens.

## Role in this knowledge base
A tool that provides extended and virtually unlimited usage for Claude Code.

## Key facts
- Offers up to 1.6 billion free tokens per month by switching to alternative AI models when usage limits are reached.
