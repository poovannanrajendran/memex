## Overview
A framework that provides simple abstractions for creating AI agents, including single- and multi-agent patterns. It was a pioneering technology in AI agents and multi-agent orchestration.

## Role in this knowledge base
A foundational technology merged into the Microsoft Agent Framework.

## Key facts
- Known for its simple agent abstractions.
