## Overview
Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) is a political party founded by popular Tamil film actor Thalapathy Vijay. The party was officially launched in February 2024, with Vijay announcing his intention to contest the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly elections.

## Role in this knowledge base
