## Overview
Communist Party of India (Marxist) is a major Indian political party with a significant presence in certain states, including Kerala and West Bengal. It has a smaller presence in Tamil Nadu.

## Role in this knowledge base
Used as a reference point for NTK's electoral performance, suggesting NTK ranks below CPM in terms of vote share.

## Key facts
- It is one of the eight recognised national parties in India.
