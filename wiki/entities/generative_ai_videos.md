## Overview
Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can create new content, such as text, images, music, and videos, based on the data they have been trained on. Generative AI videos are video content produced using these AI technologies.

## Role in this knowledge base
A specific type of AI-generated content that the bootcamp will teach participants how to create, including ads and digital twins.
