## Overview
The Open Textbook Library is a resource that curates and provides access to free, openly licensed textbooks. It aims to make high-quality educational materials more accessible to students and educators.

## Role in this knowledge base
One of the publishers whose books can now be uploaded to Google NotebookLM.

## Key facts
- It offers a collection of peer-reviewed, openly licensed textbooks.
