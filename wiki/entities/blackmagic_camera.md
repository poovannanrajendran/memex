## Overview
A professional mobile filmmaking application that provides advanced camera controls and recording capabilities on smartphones.

## Role in this knowledge base
Cited as an example of a professional app that the iPhone 18 Pro's new controls aim to simplify access to for creators.

## Key facts
- Blackmagic Camera offers features traditionally found in professional cinema cameras, such as LOG recording and advanced colour grading.
