## Overview
All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK) is another major Dravidian political party in Tamil Nadu, India, and a historical rival of the DMK. It was founded by M. G. Ramachandran.

## Role in this knowledge base
One of the two major Dravidian parties whose electoral prospects are allegedly influenced by NTK's vote splitting.

## Key facts
- It has been a dominant force in Tamil Nadu politics for decades, often forming the ruling government.
