## Overview
Naam Tamilar Katchi (NTK) is a Tamil nationalist political party in Tamil Nadu. Led by Seeman, the party advocates for Tamil self-determination and has a distinct platform focused on Tamil identity and issues. It is known for its strong stance on various social and political matters.

## Role in this knowledge base
The party is the central focus of the critique in the transcript, accused of election malpractice, vote-splitting, and operating as a 'business' for political parties.
