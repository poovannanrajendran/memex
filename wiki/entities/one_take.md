## Overview
One Take is a technique or tool that creates videos with a single, continuous camera shot that never cuts. It is available for free for non-commercial use.

## Role in this knowledge base
It is presented as a distinct style of video production emphasizing continuity.

## Key facts
- It uses a single, uninterrupted camera shot.
