## Overview
The Goods and Services Tax is an indirect tax that has replaced many central and state government indirect taxes in India. It is a comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based tax that is levied on every value addition.

## Role in this knowledge base
Used as an example of complex regulatory infrastructure in India requiring AI-native solutions.

## Key facts
- India's GST system has state-by-state rules, renewal cycles, and regulatory patchwork.
