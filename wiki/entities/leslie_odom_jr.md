## Overview
Leslie Odom Jr. is an American actor and singer. He is best known for his Tony Award-winning performance as Aaron Burr in the original Broadway production of Hamilton.

## Role in this knowledge base
Lead vocalist performing the song 'Wait for It' in the context of the musical.

## Key facts
- Won a Tony Award for Best Actor in a Musical for his role as Aaron Burr.
