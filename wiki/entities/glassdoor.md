## Overview
Glassdoor is a website where current and former employees anonymously review companies and their management. It also provides salary data and job listings. While useful for research, the video finds it less effective for direct job acquisition.

## Role in this knowledge base
A job portal recognised for salary and company culture information but not for landing offers.

## Key facts
- It is a major source for employee reviews and salary transparency.
