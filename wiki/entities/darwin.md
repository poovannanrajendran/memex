## Overview
Charles Darwin was an English naturalist, geologist, and biologist, widely known for his contributions to the science of evolution.

## Role in this knowledge base
His theory of evolution by natural selection is used as a framework to explain the impact of environmental changes on species survival.

## Key facts
- He proposed that species evolve over time through a process of natural selection.
