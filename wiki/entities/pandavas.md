## Overview
The five sons of Pandu: Yudhishthira, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula, and Sahadeva. They are the central protagonists of the Mahabharata, often in conflict with their cousins, the Kauravas.

## Role in this knowledge base
Their individual stories and collective saga form the core of the Mahabharata, with each brother contributing unique virtues and lessons.

## Key facts
- They are the central protagonists of the Mahabharata.
