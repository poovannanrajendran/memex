## Overview
Unity is a widely-used cross-platform game engine developed by Unity Technologies, enabling the creation of 2D, 3D, VR, and AR experiences.

## Role in this knowledge base
The game development environment where the game levels are built and systems are implemented.

## Key facts
- Unity is a popular choice for indie and professional game development due to its versatility and asset store.
