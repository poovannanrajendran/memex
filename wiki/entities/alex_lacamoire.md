## Overview
Alex Lacamoire is an American composer, arranger, orchestrator, and music director. He is known for his work on several Broadway musicals, including Hamilton.

## Role in this knowledge base
Conductor, keyboardist, arranger, orchestrator, and producer for the song.

## Key facts
- He won a Tony Award for Best Orchestrations for Hamilton.
