## Overview
Royal Tunbridge Wells is a spa town in West Kent, England, known for its elegant architecture, the historic Pantiles area, and a reputation for affluence and a high quality of life. It has long been a desirable residential location.

## Role in this knowledge base
It is presented as a lifestyle rival to Tonbridge, but significantly more expensive (approx. 20% more) due to its 'Royal' status and the appeal of areas like the Pantiles.

## Key facts
- Royal Tunbridge Wells is approximately 20% more expensive than Tonbridge for a comparable 3-bedroom detached house.
