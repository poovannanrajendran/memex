## Overview
The Southwest Coast 300 (SWC300) is a 300-mile scenic driving route in the southwest of Scotland. It is promoted for its diverse scenery, including coastlines, mountains, and historical sites, and is known for potential wildlife sightings.

## Role in this knowledge base
Featured road trip destination, winner of the list.

## Key facts
- It is considered the ultimate Scottish road trip, offering diverse landscapes and the chance to see marine life like dolphins and killer whales.
