## Overview
HackerTube is a custom-built recommendation system for developer videos, created by Andrej Karpathy. It aggregates and suggests tutorials and educational content, particularly those that have gained traction and positive feedback on platforms like Hacker News.

## Role in this knowledge base
A new tool for developers seeking curated educational video content.

## Key facts
- It was developed over a single weekend by Andrej Karpathy.
