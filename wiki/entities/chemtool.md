## Overview
Chemtool is a chemical product used for cleaning automotive components. In this context, it is employed as part of the process to clean the fuel injectors.

## Role in this knowledge base
Used to clean the fuel injectors as part of the engine revival process.

## Key facts
- It was used in conjunction with an ultrasonic cleaner to service the injectors.
