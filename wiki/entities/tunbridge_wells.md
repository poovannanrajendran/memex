## Overview
A spa town in Kent, south of Tonbridge, famous for its historic Pantiles area and 'Royal' designation. It is considered a lifestyle destination but is more expensive than Tonbridge.

## Role in this knowledge base
Positioned as a lifestyle rival to Tonbridge, but with a higher cost of living and property.

## Key facts
- Tunbridge Wells is known for its 'Pantiles' area, a historic Georgian colonnade.
