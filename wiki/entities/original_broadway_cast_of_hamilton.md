## Overview
The Original Broadway Cast of Hamilton refers to the ensemble of actors and musicians who first performed the hit musical on Broadway. They are integral to the original cast recording and established the characters for future productions.

## Role in this knowledge base
Performers of the song 'Wait for It' alongside Leslie Odom Jr.

## Key facts
- This cast is celebrated for originating the iconic roles in the musical.
