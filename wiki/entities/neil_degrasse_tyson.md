## Overview
Neil deGrasse Tyson is an American astrophysicist and science communicator. He is known for his work popularising science, particularly astronomy and physics, through books, television shows, and podcasts.

## Role in this knowledge base
Featured personality in the YouTube video, providing scientific insights.

## Key facts
- He is the current Director of the Hayden Planetarium in New York City.
