## Overview
Kaggle is an online community of data scientists and machine learning practitioners, owned by Google. It hosts machine learning competitions, provides datasets, and offers notebooks for collaborative work, serving as a platform for learning and showcasing data science skills.

## Role in this knowledge base
Co-host of the agentic AI training event and platform for access.

## Key facts
- Kaggle competitions often involve real-world problems and attract a vast community, driving innovation in data science.
