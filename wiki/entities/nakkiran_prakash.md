## Overview
Nakkiran Prakash is a journalist associated with Nakkiran magazine, a prominent publication in Tamil Nadu. He has been arrested for allegedly making defamatory remarks about journalist Malathi.

## Role in this knowledge base
The alleged perpetrator of defamation.

## Key facts
- He was arrested by the Chennai city police from a woman's house in Chintadripet.
