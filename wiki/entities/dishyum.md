## Overview
Dishyum is a Tamil film starring and scored by Vijay Antony. The film was directed by Sasi.

## Role in this knowledge base
Film for which songs were initially composed by Vijay Antony.

## Key facts
- Some of its songs were later used in the film Sukran.
