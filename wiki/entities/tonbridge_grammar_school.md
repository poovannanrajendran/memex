## Overview
Tonbridge Grammar School is a selective grammar school in Tonbridge, Kent, admitting both boys and girls, although it historically focused on girls. It is known for its strong academic performance and diverse curriculum.

## Role in this knowledge base
It is mentioned as one of the elite educational institutions in Tonbridge, attracting families prioritizing academic achievement for their children.

## Key facts
- It is a selective grammar school that offers a strong academic programme and contributes to the town's educational reputation.
