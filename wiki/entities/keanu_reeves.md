## Overview
Keanu Reeves is a Canadian actor widely known for his roles in the 'Matrix' and 'John Wick' film series, as well as 'Speed' and 'Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure'.

## Role in this knowledge base
Starred in '47 Ronin' and wrote a letter to the judge in support of Carl Erik Rinsch.

## Key facts
- Penned a letter to the judge in Carl Erik Rinsch's trial seeking leniency.
