## Overview
ZipString was the original toy from which the ZipStringer Arachnid evolved. It was featured on the TV show Shark Tank and in the movie Weapons.

## Role in this knowledge base
The precursor product that led to the development of the ZipStringer Arachnid.

## Key facts
- Its string length measurements were based on the inventor's arm length, referred to as 'Stevens'.
