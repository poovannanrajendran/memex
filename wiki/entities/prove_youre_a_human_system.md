## Overview
Technology designed to differentiate between human users and artificial intelligence. This is becoming increasingly important to prevent fraud, scams, and misuse of online services.

## Role in this knowledge base
Highlights the growing problem of AI-driven scams and the need for reliable identity verification.

## Key facts
- Crucial for protecting users, particularly older demographics less familiar with advanced technology, from sophisticated online fraud.
