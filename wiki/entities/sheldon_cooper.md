## Overview
Sheldon Cooper is a child prodigy with exceptional intelligence but significant deficits in social skills and empathy. He approaches situations with extreme logical analysis and often misses social cues, leading to humorous and sometimes awkward interactions.

## Role in this knowledge base
The central character whose curiosity and behaviour drive the events of the clip.

## Key facts
- He is known for his highly literal interpretation of language and social conventions.
