## Overview
An iPad application that was named Apple's iPad app of the year. It has integrated six of Desert Ant Labs' offline AI models.

## Role in this knowledge base
A case study demonstrating the successful implementation and benefits of using offline AI models for video editing.

## Key facts
- Detail replaced its cloud-based AI with local models, reducing monthly costs from thousands of euros to zero for network calls.
