## Overview
Sasi is a Tamil film director known for his work in films like 'Roja Kootam', 'Dishyum', and 'Sukran'. He often explores themes of love and relationships in his films.

## Role in this knowledge base
Director who collaborated with Vijay Antony and discusses their musical work.

## Key facts
- He initially went into cinema wanting to make films without songs.
