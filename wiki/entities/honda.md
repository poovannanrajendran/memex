## Overview
Honda Motor Company, Ltd. is a Japanese public multinational conglomerate corporation primarily known for manufacturing automobiles, motorcycles, and power equipment. It has expanded into aviation with the development of private jets.

## Role in this knowledge base
Manufacturer of the Honda Jet Elite 2, showcasing an innovative aircraft design.

## Key facts
- Honda is the world's largest manufacturer of motorcycles.
