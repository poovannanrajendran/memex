## Overview
47 Ronin is a 2013 American supernatural action film directed by Carl Erik Rinsch and starring Keanu Reeves. It is a fictionalised account of the historical revenge of the forty-seven rōnin.

## Role in this knowledge base
The film directed by Carl Erik Rinsch that gained him attention and led to his Netflix deal.
