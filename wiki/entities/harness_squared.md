## Overview
Harness Squared is an open-source AI tool developed by Google that automates the self-improvement process for AI agents. It manages prompts, tools, memory, and skills, and iteratively refines an agent's configuration to enhance its performance.

## Role in this knowledge base
The primary subject of the video, presented as a groundbreaking tool for AI agent development.

## Key facts
- It can improve AI agent scores by up to 11.5 points in a single round of automated upgrades.
