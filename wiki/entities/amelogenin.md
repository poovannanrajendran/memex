## Overview
Amelogenin is a protein crucial for the formation of tooth enamel during dental development. It acts as a scaffold, guiding the crystallisation of minerals into the hard, protective outer layer of teeth.

## Role in this knowledge base
It is the natural protein that the synthetic peptides in the new gel are designed to mimic.

## Key facts
- Amelogenin is the most abundant protein in developing enamel.
