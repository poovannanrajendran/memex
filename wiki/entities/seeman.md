## Overview
Seeman is a Tamil film director, politician, and the founder and chief coordinator of the Naam Tamilar Katchi (NTK). He is known for his strong advocacy of Tamil nationalism and often makes controversial statements.

## Role in this knowledge base
The leader of NTK, implicitly criticised through the discussion of the party's controversial rhetoric and business practices.
