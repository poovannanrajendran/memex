## Overview
Jawaharlal Nehru was the first Prime Minister of India, serving from 1947 until his death in 1964. He was a central figure in the Indian independence movement and in the shaping of modern India.

## Role in this knowledge base
Mentioned for his view of large dams as "modern temples" and his socialist leanings influencing development policy.

## Key facts
- Considered large dams to be "modern temples" of India.
