## Overview
Nakkiran is a well-established magazine in Tamil Nadu, known for its journalism. A journalist from this publication, Prakash, is at the centre of a defamation case.

## Role in this knowledge base
The media organisation employing the accused journalist.

## Key facts
- It is a publication with over 30 years of presence in the Tamil journalism landscape.
