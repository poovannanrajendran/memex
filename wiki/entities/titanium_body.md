## Overview
The use of titanium in the iPhone's construction is highlighted as a marketing tactic. The reviewer points out the perceived inconsistency in Apple promoting both the thermal efficiency of aluminum and the durability of titanium simultaneously, suggesting it's a way to create perceived value and differentiation.

## Role in this knowledge base
Cited as an example of Apple's marketing prowess, where materials are promoted to create desire and justify upgrades.

## Key facts
- Titanium is known for its high strength-to-weight ratio and corrosion resistance, making it a premium material for electronics.
