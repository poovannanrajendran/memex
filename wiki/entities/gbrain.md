## Overview
Gbrain is Garry Tan's personal AI brain, an open-sourced system designed to manage and process vast amounts of personal data. It uses an AI agent to organise information and surface insights.

## Role in this knowledge base
A tool for personal knowledge management and business operations, open-sourced by Garry Tan.

## Key facts
- It processes personal data such as meetings, emails, and company evaluations using an AI agent.
