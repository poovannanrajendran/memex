## Overview
The Pembrokeshire Coast National Park in Wales boasts a stunningly beautiful coastline, approximately 200 miles in length. It is characterised by golden beaches, rugged sea cliffs, and a famous coastal path. It is ideal for scenic drives and outdoor activities.
