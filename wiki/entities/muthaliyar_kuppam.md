## Overview
A scenic location situated along the East Coast Road (ECR) in Tamil Nadu, known for its natural beauty where the sea meets the backwaters. It is described as a place of significant aesthetic appeal.

## Role in this knowledge base
Used as a specific example of a beautiful, yet neglected, tourist spot within Tamil Nadu.

## Key facts
- It is characterised by the confluence of the sea and backwaters, creating a unique geographical feature.
