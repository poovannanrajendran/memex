## Overview
An historic independent boarding and day school for boys founded in 1553, located in Tonbridge, Kent. It is known for its academic excellence and significant presence in the town.

## Role in this knowledge base
A key factor driving Tonbridge's attractiveness as a place to live due to its prestige and the investment it brings to the town.

## Key facts
- Founded in 1553, it is one of the oldest schools in England.
