## Overview
Carfax is a commercial vehicle history service company. It collects and provides information on motor vehicle history, including accident reports, title issues, odometer readings, and service records, based on data from various sources.

## Role in this knowledge base
Provided a vehicle history report that assured the buyer of a clean title, contradicting Tesla's internal system.

## Key facts
- Carfax reports are often used by consumers to assess the condition and history of used vehicles.
