## Overview
Vijay Antony is an Indian music composer, singer, and actor who primarily works in Tamil cinema. He started his career as a music composer and later gained recognition as an actor.

## Role in this knowledge base
Music composer and actor whose songs were used in Director Sasi's film.

## Key facts
- He transitioned from being a sound engineer to a music composer and then an actor.
