## Overview
OpenStax is an educational initiative based at Rice University that provides free, peer-reviewed, openly licensed textbooks for college and Advanced Placement courses. Their mission is to improve access to education through open educational resources.

## Role in this knowledge base
One of the publishers whose books can now be uploaded to Google NotebookLM.

## Key facts
- It is a provider of free, openly licensed textbooks.
