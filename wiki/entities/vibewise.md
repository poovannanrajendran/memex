## Overview
Vibewise is a free plugin that modifies the interaction with AI coding assistants like Claude. Its primary function is to ensure developers continue to learn by prompting the AI to ask clarifying questions and provide explanations throughout the coding process.

## Role in this knowledge base
The central subject of the video, presented as a solution to the learning deficit caused by direct AI coding.

## Key facts
- It integrates with Claude Code and Codex.
