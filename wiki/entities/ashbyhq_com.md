## Overview
AshbyHQ is a modern applicant tracking system (ATS) designed to help companies manage their hiring process. It offers features for job posting, candidate sourcing, and interview scheduling.

## Role in this knowledge base
Mentioned as an example of a hiring platform where users can directly search for job openings, bypassing LinkedIn.

## Key facts
- Many companies use AshbyHQ.com as their primary job board.
