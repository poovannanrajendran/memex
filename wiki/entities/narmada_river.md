## Overview
The Narmada is one of the major rivers in India, flowing through Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, and Maharashtra. It has been the subject of significant controversy regarding the construction of large dams, most notably the Sardar Sarovar Dam.

## Role in this knowledge base
Cited as an example of opposition to large dam projects and the associated environmental and social issues.

## Key facts
- The Narmada Valley struggles faced significant resistance from environmental and human rights activists.
