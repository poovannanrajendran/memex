## Overview
Honcho is a system or platform that enables multiple AI agents to share a common memory. It reasons over user interactions to learn preferences and ongoing projects, carrying this context into subsequent conversations with any of the connected agents.

## Role in this knowledge base
The underlying technology that allows different AI agents to share context and learn from user interactions.

## Key facts
- It enables agents to learn user preferences and what they are building, bringing that into the next conversation.
