## Overview
Jawaharlal Nehru was the first Prime Minister of India and a central figure in the Indian independence movement. He advocated for industrialisation and modern infrastructure.

## Role in this knowledge base
His vision of dams as 'modern temples' and his socialist ideals are contrasted with current profit-driven development.

## Key facts
- He promoted the idea of large-scale infrastructure projects as essential for India's development.
