## Overview
Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, widely known as Thalapathy Vijay, is a leading actor in the Tamil film industry and a prominent political figure. He recently launched his political party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), signalling his entry into electoral politics.
