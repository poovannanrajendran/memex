## Overview
A system or platform that uses Artificial Intelligence to help businesses manage and adhere to complex regulatory requirements. This includes handling diverse rules, renewal cycles, and legal frameworks.

## Role in this knowledge base
Addresses a significant business challenge in India due to its intricate tax and regulatory systems.

## Key facts
- Aims to simplify adherence to India's Goods and Services Tax (GST) and other state-specific regulations.
