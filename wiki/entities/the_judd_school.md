## Overview
A boys' super-selective grammar school located in Tonbridge, Kent, consistently ranked among the top state schools in England.

## Role in this knowledge base
A primary driver for families relocating to Tonbridge, attracted by its high academic standards.

## Key facts
- It is a 'super-selective' grammar school, meaning admission is based solely on academic performance.
