## Overview
An AI Solutions Architect designs and oversees the integration of artificial intelligence systems into a company's existing technological framework. They focus on the high-level architecture and ensure that AI solutions align with business objectives and technical capabilities.

## Role in this knowledge base
The source explains the responsibilities and required skills of this role, contrasting it with a Forward Deployed Engineer.

## Key facts
- Focuses on the design and strategic integration of AI within a company's tech stack.
