## Overview
Claude Motion is a feature within Claude that transforms text prompts into animations. It operates by generating code that dictates the animation's elements, timing, and appearance, offering a high degree of editability.

## Role in this knowledge base
The core new feature being demonstrated and explained in the video.

## Key facts
- It enables users to edit specific components of the animation, such as text or scene duration, after generation.
