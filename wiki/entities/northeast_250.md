## Overview
The Northeast 250 is a scenic driving route in Scotland, covering 250 miles. It is promoted as a way to explore the best of the northeast of Scotland, encompassing mountains, distilleries, and picturesque landscapes.

## Role in this knowledge base

## Key facts
