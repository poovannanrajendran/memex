## Overview
Lin-Manuel Miranda is an American playwright, composer, lyricist, and actor. He is the creator of the Broadway musicals In the Heights and Hamilton.

## Role in this knowledge base
Composer, lyricist, and producer for the musical Hamilton and the song 'Wait for It'.

## Key facts
- He also originated the title role of Alexander Hamilton in the Broadway production.
