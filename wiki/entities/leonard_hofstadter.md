## Overview
Leonard Hofstadter is Sheldon's roommate and friend, often acting as a buffer between Sheldon and the outside world. He is more socially adept than Sheldon but still finds Sheldon's behaviour challenging.

## Role in this knowledge base
Penny's romantic interest, whose unexpected return is revealed through Sheldon's investigation.

## Key facts
- He shares a close friendship and living space with Sheldon.
