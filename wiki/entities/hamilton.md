## Overview
Hamilton: An American Musical is a hugely successful musical with music, lyrics, and book by Lin-Manuel Miranda. It chronicles the life of American Founding Father Alexander Hamilton.

## Role in this knowledge base
The musical from which the song 'Wait for It' originates.

## Key facts
- Won 11 Tony Awards, including Best Musical.
