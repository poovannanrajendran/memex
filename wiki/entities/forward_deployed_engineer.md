## Overview
A Forward Deployed Engineer is responsible for taking AI solutions and implementing them directly into a customer's specific environment. They are hands-on implementers who ensure that the designed solutions function correctly and meet the client's needs.

## Role in this knowledge base
The source defines this role, highlighting its practical, implementation-focused nature and differentiating it from the architectural role.

## Key facts
- Implements AI solutions directly in customer environments, focusing on turning plans into working products.
