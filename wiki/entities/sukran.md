## Overview
Sukran is a Tamil film directed by Sasi and starring Vijay Antony. It was released before Dishyum.

## Role in this knowledge base
Film for which Director Sasi used songs originally composed for Dishyum.

## Key facts
- It was the second film Sasi directed that featured Vijay Antony as a lead actor.
