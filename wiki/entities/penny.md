## Overview
Penny is Sheldon's neighbour and a friend who often finds herself bewildered by Sheldon's unique perspective. She represents a more socially conventional individual navigating a friendship with Sheldon.

## Role in this knowledge base
Sheldon's friend and the person whose private activities are investigated by Sheldon.

## Key facts
- She often tries to maintain normal social interactions despite Sheldon's eccentricities.
