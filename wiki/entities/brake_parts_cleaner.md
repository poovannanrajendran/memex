## Overview
Brake parts cleaner is a solvent used for cleaning automotive brake components. In this video, it is used in an unconventional method to heat spark plugs.

## Role in this knowledge base
Used in a risky procedure to heat spark plugs in an attempt to start the engine.

## Key facts
- The method involving heating spark plugs with this cleaner proved surprisingly effective, albeit temporarily.
