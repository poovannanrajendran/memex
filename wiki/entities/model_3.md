## Overview
The Tesla Model 3 is a compact executive electric sedan manufactured by Tesla, Inc. It is Tesla's first mass-market electric car.

## Role in this knowledge base
The specific model of car purchased by the buyer which turned out to have a salvage title.

## Key facts
