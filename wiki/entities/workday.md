## Overview
Workday is a cloud-based enterprise management software that offers human capital management (HCM) and financial management applications. Its recruitment module is used by large organisations to manage hiring.

## Role in this knowledge base
Identified as a hiring platform where job seekers can find opportunities beyond LinkedIn.

## Key facts
- Workday is predominantly used by larger enterprises.
