## Overview
Slack is a business communication platform that OpenClaw supports. Individual Slack workspaces or accounts can be mapped to specific agents for tailored interactions.

## Role in this knowledge base
An example of a communication channel that can be integrated with OpenClaw.

## Key facts
- Can be used with OpenClaw to route messages to specific agents based on workspace or account.
