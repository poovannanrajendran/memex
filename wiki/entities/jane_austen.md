## Overview
A renowned English novelist known for her six major novels, which interpret, critique and comment upon the British landed gentry at the end of the 18th century. Her father, George Austen, was educated at Tonbridge School.

## Role in this knowledge base
Mentioned to highlight the historical significance and pedigree associated with Tonbridge School.

## Key facts
