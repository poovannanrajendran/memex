## Overview
BlueRails is a tool designed to enhance a brand's visibility and citations within large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. It aims to help businesses get discovered when users interact with these AI platforms.

## Role in this knowledge base
The primary subject of the video, presented as a solution for brands seeking presence in AI chatbot responses.

## Key facts
- It provides an audit to suggest how to increase brand citations.
