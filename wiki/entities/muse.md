## Overview
Muse is an AI tool reviewed for its video editing capabilities. It is noted for its ability to handle raw video downloads and create short-form content with punchy cuts and a simple design. However, it was found to be less effective for thumbnail generation.

## Role in this knowledge base
The primary subject of comparison for AI video editing, specifically for short-form content creation.

## Key facts
- Successfully downloaded and edited raw video footage into short clips, outperforming other AI tools in this specific task.
