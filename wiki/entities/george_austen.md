## Overview
George Austen was the father of the renowned novelist Jane Austen. He was educated at Tonbridge School and later served as rector of the parish of Deane in Hampshire.

## Role in this knowledge base
His education at Tonbridge School is cited as a point of historical significance and prestige for the town.

## Key facts
- He was educated at Tonbridge School, a fact that lends historical weight to the town's educational legacy.
