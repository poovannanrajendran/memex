## Overview
Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK) is one of the major political parties in Tamil Nadu, India. It is a Dravidian party with a long history in the state's politics. The party generally advocates for social justice, secularism, and regional autonomy.

## Role in this knowledge base
One of the two major Dravidian parties whose electoral prospects are allegedly influenced by NTK's vote splitting.

## Key facts
- It is a principal opposition party in Tamil Nadu.
