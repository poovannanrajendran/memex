## Overview
Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (ADMK) is another major Dravidian political party in Tamil Nadu. It has historically been a rival to the DMK.

## Role in this knowledge base
Mentioned alongside DMK as a party allegedly involved in vote-selling with NTK.

## Key facts
- Split from DMK and was founded by M. G. Ramachandran.
