## Overview
Y Combinator Jobs is a platform associated with the startup accelerator Y Combinator, providing a space for startups to list job openings and for candidates to find roles within these new ventures. Founders can directly contact applicants.

## Role in this knowledge base
An underrated platform for startup roles, allowing direct founder contact.

## Key facts
- It is a platform often used by startups graduating from Y Combinator.
