## Definition
An AI memory system refers to mechanisms or architectures that enable artificial intelligence models to store, retrieve, and utilise past information or experiences. This allows AI to maintain context across interactions, learn from previous tasks, and avoid repeating errors.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for developing more sophisticated and context-aware AI assistants, like Claude Code, that can perform complex tasks efficiently without constant re-prompting.

## Key properties or components
- Storage of past interactions
- Contextual recall
- Learning from experience
- Reduction of repetitive errors

## Contradictions or debates
The effectiveness and efficiency of different memory system architectures can vary, and there are ongoing debates about the best methods for long-term memory and knowledge consolidation in AI.
