## Definition
The unique character, atmosphere, and attractiveness of a town, shaped by its history, amenities, community, and environment. This influences its desirability for residents and property buyers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Tonbridge is presented as evolving from being overshadowed by its neighbours to having a distinct and appealing identity rooted in history, community, and practicality.

## Key properties or components
- Historical landmarks (Castle)
- Riverfront lifestyle
- Independent shops/bars
- Active/unpretentious vibe
- Green spaces

## Contradictions or debates
Some may find its practicality less appealing than the 'café culture' of places like Royal Tunbridge Wells.
