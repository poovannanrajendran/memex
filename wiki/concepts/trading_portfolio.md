## Definition
A trading portfolio is a collection of financial assets, such as stocks, bonds, options, and other securities, held by an individual or institution with the primary goal of generating short-to-medium term profits through active trading strategies.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
For Poovi's Second Brain, understanding trading portfolios is key to grasping how financial assets are managed for profit, especially in conjunction with AI-driven strategies and risk assessment within the insurance and finance sectors.

## Key properties or components
- Asset allocation
- Diversification
- Risk assessment
- Profitability targets
