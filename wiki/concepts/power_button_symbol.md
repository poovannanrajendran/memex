## Definition
The power button symbol, often represented by a circle with a vertical line, indicates the function of turning a device on or off. It's a standardised icon used globally.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding the meaning of the power button symbol is fundamental for operating electronic devices and is relevant to everyday technology use.

## Key properties or components
- Circle and vertical line design
- Indicates on/off function
- Universal recognition
