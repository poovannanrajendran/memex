## Definition
The degree of skill and competence an individual possesses in understanding, using, and applying Artificial Intelligence tools and concepts effectively.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for leveraging AI's potential in various domains, from professional tasks to personal productivity.

## Key properties or components
- Knowledge of AI concepts
- Skill in using AI tools
- Ability to apply AI solutions
- Adaptability to new AI advancements
