## Definition
A business model where goods are sold in large quantities, typically to retailers or other businesses, rather than directly to individual consumers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Explains the primary sales strategy of the peanut candy business, allowing for larger production volumes and wider distribution.

## Key properties or components
- Bulk sales
- Lower per-unit price
- Business-to-business (B2B) focus
- Requires logistics for delivery

## Contradictions or debates
While primarily wholesale, the business does serve retail customers who come directly to their premises.
