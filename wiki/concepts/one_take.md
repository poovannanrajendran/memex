## Definition
A video style characterised by a single, uninterrupted camera shot that never cuts. It is available for free for non-commercial use.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Offers a distinct aesthetic of continuity and realism, achievable with AI tools.

## Key properties or components
- continuous camera
- no cuts
- free for non-commercial use

## Contradictions or debates
None.
