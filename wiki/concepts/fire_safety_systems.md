## Definition
A combination of detection, alarm, and suppression equipment designed to prevent fires from starting, alert occupants in case of a fire, and control or extinguish fires when they occur.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Vital for protecting lives and property. The failure of these systems, as discovered in the building, represents a critical safety hazard and a breach of legal requirements.

## Key properties or components
- Fire detection (smoke detectors)
- Fire alarms
- Sprinkler systems
- Emergency exits
- Water supply for suppression
