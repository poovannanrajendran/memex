## Definition
AI model collaboration involves two or more artificial intelligence models working together to achieve a common goal. This can involve sharing tasks, data, or processing capabilities.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables the creation of more powerful and efficient AI systems by combining the strengths of different models, reducing individual model load and potentially costs, as demonstrated by the Astra-Claude Code integration.

## Key properties or components
- Task delegation
- Complementary strengths
- Resource optimisation
- Error checking/correction

## Contradictions or debates
Coordinating multiple AI models can introduce complexities in communication, data synchronisation, and overall system management, which might outweigh the benefits in simpler applications.
