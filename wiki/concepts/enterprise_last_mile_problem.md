## Definition
The 'enterprise last-mile problem' refers to the challenge of taking nascent AI demonstrations or prototypes and successfully integrating them into complex, legacy enterprise systems to create scalable, compliant, and deeply integrated production solutions.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Highlights the unique challenge that FDEs address, differentiating their role from that of researchers or AI developers by focusing on the practical, difficult final steps of deployment and integration within the enterprise context.

## Key properties or components
- Bridging gap between demos and production
- Integrating into complex/messy stacks
- Ensuring scalability and compliance
- Deep integration with existing systems
