## Definition
Token waste refers to the inefficient or unnecessary consumption of tokens when interacting with AI models, particularly large language models. Tokens are the fundamental units of text that AI models process, and excessive token usage leads to higher costs and slower processing times.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Reducing token waste is crucial for managing the operational costs of AI applications like Claude Code and improving their efficiency.

## Key properties or components
- Inefficient processing
- Increased costs
- Slower response times
- Redundant data processing
