## Definition
The process by which artificial intelligence systems analyse spoken language (dictation) to understand its content, identify potential errors, and interpret the user's intent. This often involves multiple stages of verification and matching.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for ensuring that AI tools accurately capture user commands, especially in voice-controlled applications like task management.

## Key properties or components
- Speech recognition
- Error correction
- Intent recognition
- Contextual matching
- Multi-layered analysis
