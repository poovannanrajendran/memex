## Definition
This is a proactive job application strategy where a candidate proposes a specific role and outlines how they can bring value to a company, particularly when no formal opening exists. It goes beyond submitting a standard resume.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Pitching a role demonstrates initiative, research, and a clear understanding of a company's needs, setting candidates apart from those who only apply to advertised positions. It highlights the candidate's ability to identify and create value.

## Key properties or components
- Proactive approach
- Value proposition
- Customised proposal
- Demonstrates initiative

## Contradictions or debates
Some might consider this approach overly aggressive or presumptuous, but the video argues it's a powerful differentiator when executed effectively.
