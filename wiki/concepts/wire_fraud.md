## Definition
Wire fraud is a criminal offense involving the use of electronic communications, such as the internet or telephone, to carry out a fraudulent scheme. This can include misrepresenting facts or making false promises to obtain money or property.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It is the specific crime Carl Erik Rinsch was convicted of for misusing Netflix funds through electronic transfers and transactions.

## Key properties or components
- Use of wire communications
- Intent to defraud
- Obtaining money or property
- Interstate or international commerce
