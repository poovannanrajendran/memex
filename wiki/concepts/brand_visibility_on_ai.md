## Definition
Brand visibility on AI refers to the extent to which a brand's name, products, or services are recognised and appear within the outputs and interactions generated by artificial intelligence systems, particularly large language models and chatbots.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
In the context of Poovi's Second Brain, increasing brand visibility on AI platforms is crucial for staying relevant and competitive as users increasingly rely on AI for information and decision-making. Tools like BlueRaiils are presented as methods to achieve this.

## Key properties or components
- Appearing in AI-generated responses
- Increased citations in AI outputs
- Discoverability through AI queries
- Brand presence in chatbot interactions
