## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence algorithms to automatically create memes based on user inputs, such as text descriptions or image templates. This process typically involves natural language processing and image synthesis or manipulation.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Streamlines content creation, especially for social media, by automating a creative process and enabling rapid generation of engaging material.

- Automated content creation
