## Definition
In the context of AI agents, orchestration refers to the management and coordination of multiple agents or tasks to achieve a complex goal. It involves defining workflows, managing dependencies, and ensuring seamless interaction between different components.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Effective orchestration is vital for building robust and scalable AI agent systems, enabling them to handle intricate processes and collaborate efficiently.

## Key properties or components
- Workflow management
- Task sequencing
- Agent coordination
- State management
- Error handling

## Contradictions or debates
Over-orchestration can lead to rigidity and reduced adaptability in agent behaviour, while under-orchestration can result in chaos and inefficiency.
