## Definition
A project management approach that emphasises iterative development, collaboration, and flexibility. Tasks are broken down into smaller increments, with frequent reviews and adjustments to ensure the final product meets evolving requirements.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Applying agile principles to AI interaction, such as 'agile specking', allows for continuous feedback and course correction, leading to better alignment between AI output and human goals.

## Key properties or components
- Iterative development
- Small increments
- Continuous feedback
- Adaptability

## Contradictions or debates
Can be perceived as less efficient for projects with very stable and well-defined requirements from the outset, where a waterfall approach might suffice. However, with AI's inherent unpredictability, agility is often preferred.
