## Definition
XR is an umbrella term that encompasses virtual reality (VR), augmented reality (AR), and mixed reality (MR). It refers to all real-and-virtual combined environments and human-machine interactions generated by computer technology and wearables.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
XR technology is at the forefront of immersive computing, with potential applications in entertainment, education, training, and professional collaboration, aligning with Poovi's interest in cutting-edge technology.

## Key properties or components
- Combines real and virtual worlds
- Offers immersive experiences
- Includes VR, AR, and MR
