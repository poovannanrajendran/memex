## Definition
Wireframing is the process of creating a basic visual guide or blueprint for a website or application. It focuses on the layout, structure, and content placement rather than visual design elements like colours and typography.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Wireframing is crucial for establishing the foundational structure of a digital product, ensuring usability and functionality before detailed design work begins. It helps in communicating design ideas efficiently.

## Key properties or components
- Low-fidelity representation
- Focus on structure and layout
- Content placement
- User flow mapping
