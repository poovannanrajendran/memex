## Definition
AI collaboration is a paradigm where multiple artificial intelligence models or systems work together to achieve a common goal. This often involves agents with different specialisations or capabilities sharing information and tasks to improve overall performance, efficiency, or outcome.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables the combination of strengths from different AI models, leading to more complex problem-solving capabilities and reduced resource consumption by distributing workload.

## Key properties or components
- Task delegation
- Information sharing
- Complementary capabilities
- Synergistic output

## Contradictions or debates
Potential for increased complexity in management and communication overhead between models.
