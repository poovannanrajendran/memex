## Definition
Persistence in the context of AI refers to the ability of an AI model or system to retain information, state, or learned knowledge across multiple sessions or operational cycles. It ensures that data is not lost when the system is shut down or restarted.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for building AI tools that can maintain project context and user preferences over time, such as the memory provided by Agent Memory for coding assistants.

## Key properties or components
- Data retention across sessions
- Statefulness
- Non-volatility
- Continuous learning

## Contradictions or debates
Balancing the need for persistence with data privacy and the potential for outdated information to negatively impact performance remains a challenge.
