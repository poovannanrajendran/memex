## Definition
These are text-based instructions used to guide Artificial Intelligence models in generating images that emulate a retro aesthetic. They describe the desired style, era, and elements of the image.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding and utilising trending AI retro image prompts is crucial for creators looking to leverage popular visual styles in their AI-generated art for platforms like social media.

## Key properties or components
- Text-based input for AI
- Focus on retro aesthetics (e.g., vintage colours, styles, themes)
- Describe desired visual elements
- Can be 'trending' and thus popular
