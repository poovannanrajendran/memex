## Definition
A system built from the ground up using artificial intelligence to manage and ensure adherence to complex regulatory requirements. This goes beyond traditional software by embedding AI for interpretation and proactive compliance.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Reduces the significant human resources and potential errors associated with navigating intricate and evolving legal and tax frameworks, particularly in markets like India.

## Key properties or components
- Automated regulatory interpretation
- Proactive risk assessment
- Real-time compliance monitoring
- Adaptability to regulatory changes
