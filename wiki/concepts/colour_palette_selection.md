## Definition
Colour palette selection is the process of choosing a set of colours to be used in a design project, such as a website or application. It involves considering aesthetic appeal, brand identity, and user experience.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The chosen colour palette significantly impacts the overall look and feel of a product, influencing user perception, brand recognition, and emotional response. Effective colour selection enhances usability and aesthetic appeal.

## Key properties or components
- Aesthetics
- Branding
- Accessibility
- Psychology of colour
