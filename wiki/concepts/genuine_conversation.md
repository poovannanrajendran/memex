## Definition
A genuine conversation is an authentic exchange of ideas or information between individuals, characterised by clarity, intent, and mutual understanding. It is typically conducted in a state of sobriety and with a clear purpose.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Distinguishing between genuine business conversations and casual or inebriated discussions is vital for effective networking and client acquisition. Only authentic interactions can lead to tangible business opportunities.

## Key properties or components
- Authenticity
- Clarity of intent
- Mutual understanding
- Sobriety
- Purpose-driven

## Contradictions or debates
Conversations held under the influence of alcohol or in a purely casual setting are often mistaken for productive business interactions.
