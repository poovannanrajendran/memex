## Definition
In the context of AI, decision making refers to the process by which an AI system selects a course of action from a set of alternatives to achieve a specific objective. This often involves evaluating data and applying logic or learned patterns.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video argues that efficient, high-speed decision-making is a critical bottleneck for many AI applications, and Jev is presented as a solution to this problem.

## Key properties or components
- Evaluation of options
- Goal orientation
- Speed
- Accuracy
- Cost-effectiveness

## Contradictions or debates
Traditional LLMs, designed for generative tasks, are too slow for the rapid decision-making required in many software applications.
