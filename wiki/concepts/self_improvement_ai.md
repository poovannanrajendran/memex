## Definition
In the context of AI, self-improvement refers to a system's ability to modify and enhance its own performance, code, or skills over time based on experience or feedback.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This is a key characteristic of advanced AI, enabling systems like Hermes to become more efficient and effective without constant human intervention.

## Key properties or components
- Adaptive learning
- Skill refinement
- Performance enhancement
- Autonomous development
