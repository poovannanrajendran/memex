## Definition
AI model aggregation is the practice of integrating multiple AI models from various providers into a single platform or API. This allows users to access a diverse range of AI capabilities through a unified interface.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables cost savings and improved reliability by allowing users to leverage the strengths of different models and automatically switch to alternatives if one fails or becomes too expensive.

## Key properties or components
- Multiple providers
- Unified API
- Cost optimisation
- Load balancing
- Failover mechanisms
