## Definition
User distribution refers to the ongoing strategies and channels used to consistently acquire new users for a product or service.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
A consistent flow of new users is necessary for growth, testing, and continued improvement; without it, even a good product will stagnate and fail.

## Key properties or components
- Content marketing
- Community outreach
- Repeatable acquisition channels
- Sustained growth
