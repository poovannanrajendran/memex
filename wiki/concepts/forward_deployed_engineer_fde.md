## Definition
A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is a specialised role focused on integrating advanced AI models, particularly frontier models, directly into complex enterprise production systems. They bridge the gap between AI development and real-world application within client environments.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for enterprises looking to leverage cutting-edge AI, as FDEs ensure these models are not just functional prototypes but are seamlessly integrated, secure, and compliant within existing technological infrastructures.

## Key properties or components
- Protocol Integration
- Evaluation Engineering
- Enterprise Security
- High-Velocity Prototyping
- Integration with legacy systems
- Ensuring model performance and compliance

## Contradictions or debates
The distinction between FDEs and traditional software engineers might blur as traditional roles increasingly incorporate AI elements, though the FDE role emphasises deep integration and productionisation of frontier models.
