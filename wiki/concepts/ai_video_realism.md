## Definition
The quality of an AI-generated video that makes it indistinguishable from real-world footage to a human observer. This involves replicating subtle imperfections and natural behaviours.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for the adoption and acceptance of AI-generated content, particularly in applications requiring believability, and for understanding the limitations of current AI generation tools.

## Key properties or components
- Natural skin texture (pores, redness)
- Realistic eye reflections and blinking patterns
- Consistent and plausible lighting and shadows
- Subtle camera movements and imperfections (e.g., handheld feel)
- Naturalistic human features and expressions

## Contradictions or debates
The drive for AI to achieve 'perfection' often leads to its detection, as real-world footage contains inherent flaws and imperfections.
