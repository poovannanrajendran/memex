## Definition
This involves prompting AI models to generate content directly in HyperText Markup Language (HTML), allowing for the creation of functional web pages.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video points out that LLMs are proficient at front-end development and can generate interactive web pages with features like calculators and animations when prompted correctly.
