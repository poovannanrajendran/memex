## Definition
A situation where electronic storage devices containing data are lost or stolen. This can lead to data breaches and loss of critical information.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
In the context of government inspections, missing hard disks can indicate a loss of sensitive data or an attempt to destroy evidence, raising questions of accountability and security.

## Key properties or components
- Data loss
- Security breach
- Potential evidence tampering
