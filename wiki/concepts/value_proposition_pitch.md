## Definition
A pitch that focuses on demonstrating the specific value or benefit an individual can bring to a company, rather than simply stating a desire for a job. It highlights how the applicant can solve problems or create opportunities for the employer.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This approach is presented as highly effective for standing out in a competitive job market, especially for startups, by showcasing initiative and a clear understanding of how to contribute.

## Key properties or components
- Focus on employer needs
- Highlights specific skills/solutions
- Proactive and initiative-driven
- Often includes a proposed role

## Contradictions or debates
None.
