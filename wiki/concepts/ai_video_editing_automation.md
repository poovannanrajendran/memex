## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence tools to automate various stages of video production, such as downloading footage, cutting clips, adding effects, and generating captions or thumbnails.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for streamlining content creation workflows, reducing manual effort, and increasing the speed at which short-form video content can be produced and published, particularly relevant for platforms like YouTube Shorts.

## Key properties or components
- Automated footage retrieval
- Intelligent clip selection and cutting
- Automated captioning and overlay generation
- Thumbnail creation
- Effect application (e.g., zoom, transitions)

## Contradictions or debates
While AI can automate many tasks, the quality and effectiveness can vary significantly between different tools, and human oversight or editing may still be required for optimal results.
