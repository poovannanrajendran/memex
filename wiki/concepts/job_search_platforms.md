## Definition
These are online platforms or tools that list job openings from various companies. They range from general job boards like LinkedIn to specialised applicant tracking systems (ATS) that host company-specific career pages.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding and utilising diverse job search platforms is crucial for job seekers to broaden their opportunities and reduce competition by avoiding overly saturated channels.

## Key properties or components
- Job listings
- Company career pages
- Applicant tracking systems (ATS)
- Search filters

## Contradictions or debates
While LinkedIn is a major platform, relying on it exclusively may miss numerous unlisted opportunities.
