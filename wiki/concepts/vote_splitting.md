## Definition
Vote splitting occurs when a group of voters who share similar political views cast their ballots for multiple candidates or parties, thereby dividing their potential voting bloc and potentially allowing a less-favoured candidate or party to win.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept is central to the video's argument about NTK's alleged strategy to weaken the major Dravidian parties by dividing their vote share.

## Key properties or components
- Division of a voter base
- Strategic electoral manipulation
- Impact on election outcomes
- Often benefits a third party or the main opposition
