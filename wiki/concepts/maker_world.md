## Definition
The 'Maker World' refers to the community and ecosystem of hobbyists, inventors, and DIY enthusiasts who create and build physical objects, often using digital fabrication tools and open-source hardware and software.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The Sesame robot is presented as a tool and project relevant to this community, encouraging them to build and innovate in robotics and embedded systems.

## Key properties or components
- DIY culture
- Open Source
- Prototyping
- Community collaboration
