## Definition
The practice of writing scripts specifically tailored for AI-driven video generation systems. This involves structuring content to align with the AI's ability to interpret and visualise actions.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for effective AI video generation, ensuring that the script provides clear, actionable prompts that the AI can translate into coherent visual sequences, avoiding ambiguity and improving narrative flow.

## Key properties or components
- Short sentences
- Present tense
- Action-oriented descriptions
- Focus on visual elements
- Simplicity and clarity

## Contradictions or debates
The tendency to write scripts in a conventional narrative style without considering the AI's specific interpretation mechanics, leading to disjointed or nonsensical video output.
