## Definition
Speech transcription is the process of converting spoken language into written text. Advanced systems can accurately transcribe audio, identify different speakers, and handle multiple languages, often in real-time or near real-time.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for record-keeping, content analysis, accessibility for the hearing impaired, and enabling search functionalities within audio and video content.

## Key properties or components
- Audio to text conversion
- Speaker identification
- Multi-language support
- Timestamping

## Contradictions or debates
Accuracy can be affected by background noise, accents, and the complexity of the language used. Distinguishing between multiple speakers in a crowded or overlapping conversation remains a challenge for some systems.
