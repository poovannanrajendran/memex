## Definition
Literal interpretation is the act of understanding words or statements in their most basic, dictionary definition, without considering figurative language, implied meaning, or contextual nuance. It is a hallmark of individuals with certain cognitive styles, including Sheldon Cooper.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Sheldon's literal interpretation of conversations and situations directly leads to his social blunders and misunderstandings in this clip.

## Key properties or components
- Absence of figurative language
- Focus on explicit meaning
- Lack of inferential reasoning
- Precision

## Contradictions or debates
While useful for logic and science, it can be a significant barrier in understanding human emotion and social dynamics.
