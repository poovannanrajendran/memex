## Definition
This refers to the specific internet domain name used by a company, typically ending in .com, .org, or a country-specific extension. It's also used colloquially to refer to the company's area of business or industry.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Knowing a company's domain is essential for direct outreach, enabling the sender to address emails correctly and demonstrate research into the company's field.

## Key properties or components
- Unique web address
- Indicator of industry/sector
- Used in email addresses (e.g., @company.com)
