## Definition
A technique aimed at minimising the number of 'tokens' (units of text) processed by Large Language Models (LLMs). This is crucial for managing computational costs and improving efficiency.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Directly addresses the cost and efficiency concerns associated with running advanced AI models like GPT-6 Astra, making them more accessible and sustainable for users.

## Key properties or components
- Task decomposition
- Model collaboration
- Optimised processing
- Cost management
