## Definition
A microcontroller is a small computer on a single integrated circuit containing a processor core, memory, and programmable input/output peripherals. They are designed for embedded applications.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The core component for controlling the functions of the Sesame robot and other embedded systems, enabling programmability and automation.

## Key properties or components
- Integrated processor
- Memory
- Programmable I/O
- Low power consumption
