## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence algorithms and machine learning models to automate or assist in the creation of user interfaces for software and applications. This can range from generating initial design concepts to optimising layouts and user flows.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Accelerates the design process, democratises design capabilities for non-experts, and potentially leads to more innovative and user-friendly interfaces through data-driven suggestions.

## Key properties or components
- Automation
- Generative capabilities
- Predictive analysis
- Optimization

## Contradictions or debates
None.
