## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence technologies to automate and assist in the creation of websites and web elements. This includes generating layouts, content, and design components.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Significantly boosts efficiency and reduces the time and effort required for web design, making it more accessible and faster.

## Key properties or components
- Automation of design tasks
- Content generation
- Layout suggestions
- Code generation (potentially)

## Contradictions or debates
None.
