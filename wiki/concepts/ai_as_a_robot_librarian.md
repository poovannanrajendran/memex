## Definition
An analogy for AI that highlights its reliance on its existing knowledge base (its 'library') and its inability to intrinsically know when it lacks specific information, potentially leading to fabricated outputs.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Helps to understand why AI can be confidently wrong and underscores the need for verification rather than treating it as a human with intrinsic motivation.

## Key properties or components
- Operates based on available data
- Does not know what it doesn't know
- Can confidently generate incorrect information
