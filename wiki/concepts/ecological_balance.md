## Definition
Ecological balance refers to the stability and resilience of an ecosystem, where all components (biotic and abiotic) are in equilibrium.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Maintaining ecological balance is crucial for the long-term health of the planet and human well-being, as disruptions can lead to severe environmental consequences.

## Key properties or components
- Interdependence of species
- Stable environmental conditions
- Natural cycles (e.g., water cycle)
