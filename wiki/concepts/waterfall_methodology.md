## Definition
A traditional, linear project management approach where each phase of development must be completed before the next begins. Phases typically include requirements, design, implementation, verification, and maintenance.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Contrasted with Agile in the video to highlight why a sequential, 'big task all at once' approach is less effective when working with AI, which benefits from iterative feedback.

## Key properties or components
- Linear progression
- Sequential phases
- Defined stages
- Late feedback

## Contradictions or debates
Rigid and resistant to change; issues discovered late in the process can be costly and difficult to rectify. It is generally considered less suitable for complex, evolving projects compared to Agile.
