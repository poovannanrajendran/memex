## Definition
Buoyancy is the upward force exerted by a fluid that opposes the weight of an immersed object. In simpler terms, it's the force that makes things float.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Buoyancy is crucial for understanding why enormous animals can exist in the ocean but not on land, as it counteracts gravity and allows for greater body mass.

## Key properties or components
- Upward force
- Exerted by fluids
- Opposes weight
- Depends on fluid density and object's volume
