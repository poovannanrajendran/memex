## Definition
Battery replacement in an electric vehicle involves removing the old, degraded, or damaged battery pack and installing a new or refurbished one. This is a significant and often costly repair or maintenance procedure for EVs.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The battery is the most critical and expensive component of an electric vehicle. Its health and performance directly determine the vehicle's range, charging speed, and overall functionality. A damaged or failing battery can render an EV unusable and necessitate a costly replacement.

## Key properties or components
- Expensive procedure
- Critical for EV performance
- Can be done with new or used/refurbished units
- Requires specialised knowledge and equipment
- Battery health affects resale value
