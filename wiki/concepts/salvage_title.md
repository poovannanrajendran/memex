## Definition
A salvage title is issued to a vehicle that has been declared a total loss by an insurance company due to damage, theft, or other reasons. It indicates that the cost of repairing the vehicle exceeds a certain percentage of its pre-damage market value.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
A salvage title significantly impacts a vehicle's value, insurability, and legality. It alerts potential buyers and owners to extensive prior damage, and vehicles with salvage titles often cannot be registered or driven without passing rigorous inspections.

## Key properties or components
- Indicates a vehicle was declared a total loss
- Significantly reduces vehicle value
- May prevent registration and insurance
- Requires inspection to be retitled (e.g., rebuilt title)

## Contradictions or debates
The core contradiction here is the discrepancy between the 'clean and clear title' assurance given at purchase and Tesla's internal records classifying the vehicle as 'salvage'. This highlights potential failings in vehicle history reporting or dealer transparency.
