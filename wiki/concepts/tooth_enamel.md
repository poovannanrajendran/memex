## Definition
Tooth enamel is the hard, outermost layer of a tooth, primarily composed of minerals. It is the hardest substance in the human body and serves to protect the underlying dentin and pulp from wear, decay, and thermal shock.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding and regenerating tooth enamel is crucial for dental health, preventing sensitivity, decay, and the need for more invasive dental procedures.

## Key properties or components
- Hardest substance in the human body
- Mineralised layer
- Acellular (contains no living cells)
- Susceptible to acid erosion and mechanical wear
