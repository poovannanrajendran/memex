## Definition
A systematic examination of a building's structure, systems, and safety features to ensure compliance with regulations and standards.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for verifying a building's safety, habitability, and compliance before occupancy or during tenancy.

## Key properties or components
- Structural integrity checks
- MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) system assessment
- Fire safety system evaluation
- Code compliance verification

## Contradictions or debates
A routine inspection intended to verify readiness unexpectedly uncovered severe, systemic failures.
