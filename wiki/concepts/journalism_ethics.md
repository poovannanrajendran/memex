## Definition
Journalism ethics are the moral principles and standards that guide journalists in their work, ensuring accuracy, fairness, and objectivity.

The case raises questions about the ethical conduct of journalists, particularly concerning the spread of unverified and harmful information.

- Fairness and impartiality
- Humanity
- Accountability
