## Definition
ASDSTE-100 is a controlled language standard originally developed for aerospace maintenance documentation. It aims for clarity, precision, and conciseness in technical writing.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It is presented as a prompting technique to improve the clarity and readability of AI-generated text while maintaining a natural tone, preventing outputs from sounding overly robotic.

## Key properties or components
- Controlled language
- Aerospace documentation
- Clarity
- Conciseness
- Readability

## Contradictions or debates
While strict adherence can lead to robotic sounding text, a partial application (around 80%) is suggested to balance clarity with natural language.
