## Definition
Price monitoring is the act of tracking the prices of products or services over time, often with alerts for significant changes or deals.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This function helps consumers and businesses make informed purchasing decisions and capitalise on savings opportunities, which Hermes automates.

## Key properties or components
- Price tracking
- Deal alerts
- Product specific
- Automated notifications
