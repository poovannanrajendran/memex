## Definition
In the context of technology, 'headroom' refers to the excess capacity or performance beyond what is currently required. It allows for future software updates, more demanding applications, and ensures the device remains capable and relevant for an extended period.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It justifies the purchase of current-generation hardware by ensuring longevity and future-proofing, allowing users to avoid frequent upgrades as technology advances.

## Key properties or components
- Excess processing power
- Enhanced thermal management
- Future software compatibility
- Extended device lifespan
- Ability to run future demanding applications
