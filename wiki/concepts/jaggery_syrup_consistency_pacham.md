## Definition
The specific stage or texture achieved when boiling and cooking jaggery (unrefined cane sugar) or sugar syrup. Different 'pachams' (e.g., soft ball, hard ball, crack stage) result in different textures in confectionery.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video repeatedly stresses the critical importance of achieving the correct 'pacham' for peanut candy. An incorrect consistency can lead to the candy being too soft, too hard, or having a poor texture, directly impacting the final product's quality and marketability.

## Key properties or components
- Boiling point
- Viscosity
- Texture of cooled syrup (e.g., chewy, brittle)
- Temperature control
- Skill-based judgment
- Impact on final candy texture

## Contradictions or debates
The transcript implies that achieving the correct 'pacham' is difficult and requires significant experience, suggesting that automated or less experienced methods might struggle to replicate the desired outcome consistently.
