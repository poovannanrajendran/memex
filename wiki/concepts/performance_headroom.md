## Definition
Performance headroom refers to the excess processing capacity or capability of a device or system beyond its current operational needs. It allows for future software updates, more demanding applications, and sustained performance over the device's lifespan without becoming obsolete.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept is crucial for understanding Apple's strategy of over-engineering chips to ensure iPhones remain performant and relevant for several years, mitigating the need for frequent upgrades.

## Key properties or components
- Future-proofing
- Sustained performance
- Scalability
- Extended device lifespan

## Contradictions or debates
The necessity and tangible benefit of such extensive headroom for the average user are debated, as current applications may not fully utilise it.
