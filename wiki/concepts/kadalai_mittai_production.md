## Definition
Kadalai mittai, or peanut candy, is a traditional Indian sweet made primarily from roasted peanuts and jaggery. Its production involves carefully preparing a syrup from jaggery and combining it with the peanuts, followed by cooling, shaping, and cutting into pieces.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding the traditional methods of producing Kadalai Mittai provides insight into local food industries, artisanal craftsmanship, and the cultural significance of such sweets in South India.

## Key properties or components
- Peanut-based
- Jaggery as a binder
- Requires precise syrup consistency
- Often produced manually
- Popular as a snack and for festivals
