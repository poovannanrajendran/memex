## Definition
The SDLC is a framework that outlines the stages involved in creating and maintaining software, typically including planning, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
ECC claims to automate and streamline the entire SDLC, significantly reducing the time and resources typically required.

## Key properties or components
- Planning
- Design
- Implementation (Build)
- Testing
- Security
- Deployment
- Maintenance
