## Definition
Artificial intelligence models that can perform their intended functions directly on a local device (like a smartphone or computer) without requiring a constant internet connection or connection to a remote server.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables AI capabilities on devices with limited or no connectivity, enhances user privacy by keeping data local, and can reduce latency and operational costs.

## Key properties or components
- Local execution
- No internet dependency
- Task-specific optimisation
- Reduced latency
- Enhanced privacy
