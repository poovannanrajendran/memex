## Definition
The competencies and abilities required for individuals to perform effectively in software development and related technical roles. This includes programming languages, problem-solving, system design, and specific engineering disciplines.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The core focus of Hackertube.org; the platform aims to enhance these skills by providing access to high-quality educational videos for developers.

## Key properties or components
- Technical proficiency
- Problem-solving
- System design
- Continuous learning
- Specialisation (e.g., inference engineer)
