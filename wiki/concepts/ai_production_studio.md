## Definition
A system or platform that uses artificial intelligence to automate and manage various aspects of media production, such as scripting, voiceover generation, editing, and visual creation.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Significantly lowers the barrier to entry for professional-quality video content creation, making it accessible to a wider range of users and businesses.

## Key properties or components
- AI-driven automation
- Multi-stage content generation
- Integration of various creative tasks
- Prompt-based operation
