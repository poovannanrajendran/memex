## Definition
A method by which AI models process input text, where each word or phrase is assigned a 'weight' indicating its importance. The model considers these weighted elements collectively rather than strictly adhering to sequential order or grammatical structure.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Explains why the structure and explicit phrasing within prompts are critical for AI output, and why a simple paragraph can be less effective than a block-based structure.

## Key properties or components
- Phrase importance
- Non-sequential processing
- Influence of individual terms
- Lack of narrative understanding
