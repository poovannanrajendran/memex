## Definition
The process of bypassing the safety restrictions and ethical guidelines programmed into an AI model to make it generate responses it was designed to refuse. This is achieved through carefully crafted text prompts.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It reveals fundamental limitations in current AI safety mechanisms and highlights the ongoing challenge of controlling AI behaviour, posing risks for misinformation and misuse.

## Key properties or components
- Relies solely on prompt manipulation
- Aims to circumvent AI safety protocols
- Exploits inherent model behaviours
- Demonstrates model vulnerabilities

## Contradictions or debates
The existence of jailbreaks contradicts the notion that AI safety measures are infallible or completely prevent the generation of harmful content.
