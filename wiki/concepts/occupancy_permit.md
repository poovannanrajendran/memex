## Definition
A permit that is issued by the relevant authority to allow a building, or part of a building, to be legally occupied. It signifies that the building has met all safety and zoning requirements.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Confirms that a building is safe and suitable for its intended use. The lack of an occupancy permit, as discovered by the city inspector, rendered the building illegal to occupy and use.

## Key properties or components
- Final inspection
- Code compliance verification
- Legal authorisation
