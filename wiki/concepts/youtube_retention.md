## Definition
A Claude skill that analyses a video's retention curve, providing specific timestamps and suggestions to improve viewer watch time.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Helps creators identify and fix points where viewers drop off, leading to higher overall watch time and better YouTube algorithm performance.

## Key properties or components
- Analyzes retention curve
- Provides timestamps for improvement
- Fixes retention issues
