## Definition
Task routing is the process of directing a given task to the most appropriate resource or agent for its completion. In AI systems, this often involves matching task complexity with model capability.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Key feature of Rufflow that optimises resource usage by assigning simple tasks to cheaper models and complex tasks to more powerful ones, thereby reducing costs and improving efficiency.

## Key properties or components
- Complexity assessment
- Resource allocation
- Cost optimisation
- Efficiency improvement
