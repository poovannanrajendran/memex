## Definition
An artificial intelligence system designed to provide personalised educational support to students. It can teach subjects, offer feedback, and adapt to individual learning paces.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Addresses the need for quality education in regions with high student-to-teacher ratios, offering scalable and accessible learning.

## Key properties or components
- Personalised learning paths
- Adaptive difficulty
- Feedback mechanisms
- Subject matter expertise
