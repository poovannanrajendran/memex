## Definition
Task sharing is a method where different AI models or components collaborate by dividing and distributing specific tasks among themselves. This allows each component to focus on its strengths and can improve overall efficiency and output quality.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables the combined utilisation of models like GPT-6 Astra and Claude Code, leveraging their respective capabilities while mitigating individual limitations and reducing resource consumption.

## Key properties or components
- Task decomposition
- Inter-model communication
- Collaborative processing
- Load balancing
