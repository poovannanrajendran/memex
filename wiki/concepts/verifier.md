## Definition
The verifier is the component or process within an AI interaction workflow dedicated to checking and validating the AI's output against predefined criteria to ensure accuracy, quality, and alignment with goals.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Addresses AI's limitations in grasping non-measurable aspects and its tendency to 'confidently make things up' when information is missing, thereby improving reliability.

## Key properties or components
- Setting precise evaluation criteria upfront
- Using a secondary AI model as a critic
- Pulling external signals and context for verification
