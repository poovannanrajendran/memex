## Definition
Intelligence refers to the capacity for logic, understanding, self-awareness, learning, emotional knowledge, reasoning, planning, creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving. Understanding implies a deeper grasp of context, meaning, and implications.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Karpathy emphasizes that while AI offers cheap intelligence (computational power), it is human 'understanding' of goals and context that remains irreplaceable and essential for directing AI effectively.

## Key properties or components
- Intelligence: Processing, calculation, pattern recognition
- Understanding: Contextual grasp, meaning, foresight, strategic direction
