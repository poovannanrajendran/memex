## Definition
Remote jobs are positions where employees can work from a location other than a central office, typically from home. This work model has become increasingly popular, especially with advancements in technology.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The rise of remote work offers greater flexibility, work-life balance, and access to a global talent pool, making platforms that facilitate remote job searches particularly valuable.

## Key properties or components
- Location independence
- Flexible hours
- Reduced commute
- Global opportunities

## Contradictions or debates
Challenges can include maintaining team cohesion, ensuring adequate supervision, and managing potential isolation for employees.
