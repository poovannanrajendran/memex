## Definition
A character whose traits are in contrast with those of another character (the protagonist), serving to highlight the traits of the other character. This contrast is used to illuminate specific qualities of the protagonist.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Aaron Burr serves as a foil to Alexander Hamilton, emphasizing Hamilton's impulsiveness, ambition, and revolutionary spirit through Burr's contrasting patience, caution, and desire for legacy.

## Key properties or components
- Contrast
- Highlighting protagonist's traits
- Thematic development
- Dramatic tension

## Contradictions or debates
A foil's characteristics can sometimes overshadow the protagonist if not carefully managed by the narrative.
