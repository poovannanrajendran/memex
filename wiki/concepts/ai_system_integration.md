## Definition
The process of incorporating artificial intelligence capabilities into existing software, hardware, or business processes. It involves ensuring that AI components work seamlessly with other parts of a system.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for organisations looking to leverage AI without completely overhauling their current technological infrastructure. Both roles discussed are vital for successful integration.

## Key properties or components
- Compatibility
- Scalability
- Security
- Data Flow Management
