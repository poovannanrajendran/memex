## Definition
The process of manufacturing candy made primarily from peanuts and a sugar or jaggery syrup. It involves preparing the syrup to the correct consistency, mixing it with roasted peanuts, and then shaping and cutting the mixture before it hardens.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept is central to the video, illustrating a traditional food manufacturing process and the skills involved in maintaining product quality and taste.

## Key properties or components
- Ingredient sourcing (peanuts, jaggery)
- Roasting of peanuts
- Jaggery syrup preparation (pacham/consistency)
- Mixing and forming
- Cutting and cooling
- Manual vs. automated processes
- Quality control (taste, texture)

## Contradictions or debates
None explicitly stated in the transcript, but the emphasis on manual skill suggests potential challenges in scaling production rapidly or maintaining absolute consistency without experienced labour.
