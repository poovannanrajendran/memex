## Definition
A system where users can interact with and manage their to-do lists entirely through spoken instructions. This involves natural language processing to understand commands and execute corresponding actions.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables hands-free and potentially faster management of tasks, integrating with AI tools for enhanced efficiency and accessibility.

## Key properties or components
- Natural language processing
- Task creation
- Task completion
- Task deletion
- Categorisation
- Multi-layered analysis
