## Definition
Replacing a vehicle's battery with a pre-owned but functional unit. This is often a more cost-effective option compared to purchasing a brand-new battery, particularly for older vehicles or those out of warranty.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Offers a significant cost-saving measure for repairing electric vehicles, making them more economically viable for owners facing unexpected, expensive component failures like a damaged battery.

## Key properties or components
- Lower cost than new.
- Availability can vary.
- Often comes with a limited warranty.
- Requires careful inspection and testing.
