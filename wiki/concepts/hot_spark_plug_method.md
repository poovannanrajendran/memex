## Definition
A highly unconventional and risky method for starting a stubborn engine, involving heating the spark plugs to a high temperature. This is often achieved by applying flammable liquids, such as brake cleaner, to them and igniting the fumes.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
In this specific case, the method was surprisingly effective in achieving a temporary engine start after other more conventional attempts failed. It highlights a 'last resort' scenario.

## Key properties or components
- High risk
- Use of flammable substances
- Temporary effectiveness
- Requires immediate action

## Contradictions or debates
This method is widely considered dangerous and detrimental to spark plugs and potentially other engine components. It is not a standard or recommended automotive repair technique.
