## Definition
The process of bypassing an AI model's safety protocols or ethical guidelines to elicit responses that the AI has been trained to refuse. This is achieved purely through carefully crafted user prompts without direct hacking of the system's underlying code or servers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It demonstrates practical vulnerabilities in AI safety systems, posing risks if AI models are used for malicious purposes or to generate harmful content. It also highlights the ongoing challenge for AI developers to secure their models.

## Key properties or components
- Utilises prompt engineering techniques.
- Aims to circumvent trained refusals.
- Does not involve hacking infrastructure.
- Exploits limitations in safety training.
