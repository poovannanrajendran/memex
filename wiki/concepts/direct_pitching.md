## Definition
A job application strategy where a candidate proactively contacts a company, often a startup, to propose a specific role or project they believe will add value, rather than responding to a pre-existing job opening.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This approach demonstrates initiative, research skills, and a clear understanding of the company's needs, setting the candidate apart from those who simply submit resumes.

## Key properties or components
- Company research
- Value proposition
- Role proposal
- Proactive outreach
- Demonstrated effort

## Contradictions or debates
Requires significant effort and confidence, and success is not guaranteed; companies may still prefer to hire through traditional channels.
