## Definition
Customer testimonials are positive statements from satisfied customers about a company's products or services. They serve as endorsements, helping to build credibility and persuade potential buyers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for building trust and social proof, which directly impacts business growth and sales conversions, especially in competitive markets.

## Key properties or components
- Authenticity
- Credibility
- Social Proof
- Persuasiveness
