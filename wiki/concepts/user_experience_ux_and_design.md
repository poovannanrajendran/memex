## Definition
User Experience (UX) encompasses a person's overall perceptions and feelings when interacting with a product, system, or service. Design, in this context, refers to the visual and interactive elements that shape this experience, focusing on usability, accessibility, and aesthetics.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The BAMA repository places a strong emphasis on UX and design, addressing a common weakness in AI-generated code by ensuring the resulting applications are not only functional but also visually appealing and intuitive to use.

## Key properties or components
- Usability
- Accessibility
- Aesthetics
- User satisfaction
- Intuitive interfaces
