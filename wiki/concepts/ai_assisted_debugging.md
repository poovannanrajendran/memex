## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence, such as large language models, to identify, diagnose, and potentially fix bugs or errors within software code or game logic.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept highlights a novel application of AI where Claude not only generates content but also actively participates in quality assurance by finding and fixing issues, potentially accelerating the testing phase.

## Key properties or components
- Automated Bug Reporting
- Code Analysis
- Automated Fixes
- Error Identification

## Contradictions or debates
Concerns may exist regarding the reliability and thoroughness of AI-generated fixes compared to human testers and developers.
