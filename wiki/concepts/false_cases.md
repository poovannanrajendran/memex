## Definition
Legal proceedings that are initiated without sufficient evidence or are based on fabricated charges.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Mentioned as something Malathi allegedly faced in the past, indicating potential misuse of the legal system.

## Key properties or components
- lack of evidence
- fabricated charges
- malicious intent
- legal repercussions for accuser
