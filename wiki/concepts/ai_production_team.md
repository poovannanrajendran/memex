## Definition
An AI Production Team refers to a suite of artificial intelligence tools and agents that collectively automate and execute various stages of content production, such as research, writing, voice acting, editing, and visual creation.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It signifies a paradigm shift in content creation, enabling faster, more cost-effective, and potentially scalable production of videos and other media, even for individuals with limited resources.

## Key properties or components
- Automation of multiple production roles
- Integration of various AI specialisations (writing, voice, editing)
- Single prompt or minimal input requirement
- Scalability and efficiency
