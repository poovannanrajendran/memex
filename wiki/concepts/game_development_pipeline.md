## Definition
The sequence of steps and processes involved in creating a video game, from initial concept to final release and post-launch support. This typically includes design, asset creation, programming, testing, and marketing.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding the pipeline is crucial for integrating AI tools like Claude effectively to streamline or automate parts of the process, potentially reducing development time and cost.

## Key properties or components
- Concept and Design
- Asset Creation (2D/3D)
- Programming/Scripting
- Level Design
- Testing and Debugging
- Audio Integration
- Release and Maintenance
