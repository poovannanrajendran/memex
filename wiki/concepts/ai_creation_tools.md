## Definition
These are software applications that leverage artificial intelligence to assist in the creation of various forms of content, such as text, images, music, and videos. They often automate complex or time-consuming aspects of the creative process.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The availability of free and integrated AI creation tools like Higgsfield democratises content creation, making advanced capabilities accessible to a wider audience and potentially increasing efficiency in creative workflows.

## Key properties or components
- Content generation (video, image, etc.)
- Automation of creative tasks
- Leverages machine learning models
- Can reduce production time and cost
