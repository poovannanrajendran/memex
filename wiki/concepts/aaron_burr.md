## Definition
Aaron Burr Jr. was an American politician and lawyer who served as the third vice president of the United States. He is famously known for killing his rival Alexander Hamilton in a duel.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The character and his perspective are central to the narrative of Hamilton, and 'Wait for It' provides insight into his motivations and worldview.

## Key properties or components
- Third Vice President of the US
- Killed Alexander Hamilton in a duel
- Complex historical figure

## Contradictions or debates
The musical's portrayal of Burr is a dramatised interpretation, focusing on his internal conflicts and narrative arc within the show.
