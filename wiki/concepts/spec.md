## Definition
In the context of AI interaction, a 'spec' is a detailed instruction set or blueprint that translates human understanding and goals into a format that an AI model can effectively process and act upon.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for bridging the gap between AI's computational power and human contextual understanding, ensuring AI actions align with desired outcomes.

## Key properties or components
- Uncovering the goal (not just the task)
- Agile approach (small, compartmentalised steps)
- Precision in instructions
- Critical user brainpower

## Contradictions or debates
None.
