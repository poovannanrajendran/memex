## Definition
The internationally recognised symbol used on electronic devices to indicate the power on/off state or standby mode. It typically consists of a circle with a vertical line segment intersecting it at the top.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for users to understand how to operate electronic devices and to identify power controls.

## Key properties or components
- Represents 'on' (1) and 'off' (0) states.
- Combined symbol signifies a toggle or standby function.
- Globally standardised for user interface clarity.

## Contradictions or debates
Some argue the symbol's meaning has become so ingrained that its binary origin is often forgotten, leading to potential confusion for those unfamiliar with its history.
