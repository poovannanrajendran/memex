## Definition
The process of applying digital effects to an audio signal to alter its characteristics. This can include simulating specific recording environments, microphone types, or audio quality.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Used to enhance the realism of AI-generated audio by mimicking the imperfections and acoustic properties of real-world recording conditions, thereby improving the perceived quality and context.

## Key properties or components
- EQ adjustment
- Reverb/Echo simulation
- Distortion effects
- Noise gating
- Simulating hardware limitations
