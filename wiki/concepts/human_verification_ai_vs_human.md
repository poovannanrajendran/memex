## Definition
The process and technology used to determine whether an entity interacting online is a human being or an artificial intelligence. This is crucial for security, preventing fraud, and maintaining the integrity of online platforms.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
As AI becomes more sophisticated, distinguishing humans from bots is vital to combat scams, fake accounts, and misinformation, especially in sensitive transactions or communications.

## Key properties or components
- Behavioral analysis
- Biometric identification
- Cognitive tests
- CAPTCHA alternatives
- Continuous authentication

## Contradictions or debates
Advanced AI can mimic human behaviour very effectively, making verification challenging. Overly stringent checks can frustrate legitimate users, impacting user experience.
