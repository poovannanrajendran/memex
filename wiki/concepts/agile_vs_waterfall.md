## Definition
Agile is a project management methodology that breaks tasks into small, iterative cycles with continuous feedback, while Waterfall is a linear approach where each phase must be completed before the next begins.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Applying an agile methodology to AI interactions, rather than a waterfall approach, allows for early detection of errors and better alignment with the intended goal.

## Key properties or components
- Agile: Iterative, feedback-driven, flexible scope
- Waterfall: Linear, sequential, fixed scope
