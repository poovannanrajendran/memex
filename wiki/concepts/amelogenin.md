## Definition
Amelogenin is the primary protein involved in the formation and mineralisation of tooth enamel during tooth development. It acts as a scaffold, guiding the crystallisation of minerals into the complex structure of enamel.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The synthetic gel is designed to mimic amelogenin's function, highlighting the biological pathway that the new technology aims to replicate for enamel repair.

## Key properties or components
- Major protein in enamel matrix
- Essential for enamel mineralisation
- Acts as a signalling molecule and scaffold
