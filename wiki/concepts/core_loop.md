## Definition
In game design, the core loop is the cycle of actions that the player repeats most frequently during gameplay. It's the fundamental mechanic that keeps players engaged.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Defining a clear core loop is the first step in the design process outlined, guiding the generation of game systems and ensuring a cohesive player experience.

## Key properties or components
- Actionable
- Engaging
- Repeatable
- Measurable
