## Definition
8K and 4K refer to specific high-definition video resolutions. 4K (approximately 4000 pixels horizontally) offers significantly more detail than Full HD (1080p), while 8K (approximately 8000 pixels horizontally) offers four times the detail of 4K, providing extremely sharp and lifelike images.
