## Definition
Gravity is a fundamental force of nature that attracts any two objects with mass or energy. On Earth, gravity is what keeps us grounded and affects the weight of everything.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Gravity is a significant limiting factor for the size of land animals, as their skeletons and muscles must support their entire body weight against this pull. In water, buoyancy mitigates this effect.

## Key properties or components
- Attractive force
- Acts between masses
- Weakest fundamental force but long-range
- Determines weight
