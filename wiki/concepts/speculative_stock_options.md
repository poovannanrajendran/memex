## Definition
Speculative stock options are financial derivatives that give the holder the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying asset (like stocks) at a specified price on or before a certain date, often used for high-risk, high-reward trading.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This was one of the methods Carl Erik Rinsch used to lose a significant portion of the Netflix funds entrusted to him for his TV show.

## Key properties or components
- High risk
- Potential for high reward
- Derivative financial instrument
- Time-sensitive

## Contradictions or debates
The high-risk nature of such investments is often contrasted with the fiduciary duty to safeguard funds, especially when those funds are meant for a production budget.
