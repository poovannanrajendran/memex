## Definition
The process of creating compelling and effective combinations of video titles and thumbnail images designed to attract clicks and inform potential viewers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Effective packaging is crucial for a video's discoverability and initial engagement on the YouTube platform, directly impacting viewership.

## Key properties or components
- Title generation
- Thumbnail design
- Click-worthiness
- A/B testing enablement
