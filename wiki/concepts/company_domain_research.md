## Definition
The process of investigating a company's area of operation, business model, target market, and overall mission. This is often done to understand potential needs or identify opportunities for contribution.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for effectively pitching a role or demonstrating how one can add value, especially when contacting startups directly.

## Key properties or components
- Understanding business model
- Identifying market niche
- Researching company mission
- AI-assisted analysis

## Contradictions or debates
Information might be outdated or incomplete, requiring cross-referencing from multiple sources.
