## Definition
A conceptual model, visualised as an iceberg, used to categorise individuals or entities based on their level of proficiency and usage of Artificial Intelligence tools. The majority of 'ice' is underwater, representing basic usage, while the tip represents advanced mastery.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Helps to understand the spectrum of AI tool adoption and identify areas for user development.

## Key properties or components
- Categorisation by proficiency
- Visual representation (iceberg metaphor)
- Distinction between basic and advanced usage

None.
