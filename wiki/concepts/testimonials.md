## Definition
Testimonials are statements from customers or clients endorsing a product, service, or business. They serve as a form of social proof, helping to build credibility and trust with potential customers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for building trust and credibility for a business by showcasing positive customer experiences.

## Key properties or components
- Customer endorsements
- Build trust
- Social proof
- Enhance credibility

## Contradictions or debates
None.
