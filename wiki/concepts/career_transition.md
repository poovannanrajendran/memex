## Definition
A career transition refers to the process of moving from one job, role, or industry to another. This can involve a change in responsibilities, skill sets, or even the overall nature of the work.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Kevin Hart discusses his transition from a sitcom actor ('Modern Family') to an 'action star,' illustrating a common career trajectory in the entertainment industry.

## Key properties or components
- Change in role
- Skill adaptation
- Industry shift
- Professional development
