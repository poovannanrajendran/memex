## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence agents or systems to automatically playtest a game, identify bugs, test balance, and provide feedback on the gameplay experience.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
AI playtesting can significantly speed up the testing phase, uncover bugs that human testers might miss, and provide objective feedback, leading to higher quality games.

## Key properties or components
- Autonomous Play
- Bug Detection
- Performance Metrics
- Automated Feedback
