## Definition
Job positions available within startup companies, which are typically new, rapidly growing businesses often characterised by innovation, a fast-paced environment, and potential for significant impact.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Appeals to individuals seeking dynamic work environments, opportunities to contribute significantly, and potential for equity or rapid career progression.

## Key properties or components
- Fast-paced environment
- Innovation-driven
- Potential for high impact
- Often less structured

## Contradictions or debates
Startup roles can also come with higher job insecurity, longer working hours, and lower initial compensation compared to established corporations.
