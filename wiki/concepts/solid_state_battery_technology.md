## Definition
Solid-state batteries are a type of battery that uses solid electrolytes instead of liquid or polymer electrolytes found in conventional lithium-ion batteries. They promise higher energy density, improved safety, and longer lifespan.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video explores whether this advanced battery technology can contribute to reviving a dead engine, contrasting it with older battery systems.

## Key properties or components
- Uses solid electrolytes
- Potentially higher energy density
- Improved safety
- Longer lifespan
