## Definition
The examination of a graph that illustrates how many viewers are still watching a video at different points in its duration, highlighting drop-off points.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding where viewers leave a video allows creators to pinpoint weaknesses in their content and make strategic improvements to maintain viewer engagement.

## Key properties or components
- Visualisation of viewer drop-off
- Identifies weak points in content
- Data-driven content improvement
- Maximises watch time
