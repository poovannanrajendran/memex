## Definition
The perceived worth of a property based on factors such as location, size, condition, amenities, and market demand. It involves comparing prices relative to specific benefits like commute time or school quality.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Tonbridge is positioned as offering superior value compared to its neighbours, providing a desirable combination of commute, education, and lifestyle at a more accessible price point.

## Key properties or components
- Price comparison
- Commute cost ratio
- Lifestyle premium
- Educational advantage premium
- Location desirability

## Contradictions or debates
While offering better value than Sevenoaks or Tunbridge Wells, it is still more expensive than smaller surrounding villages or towns like Paddock Wood.
