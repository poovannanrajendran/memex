## Definition
Gbrain is Garry Tan's open-sourced personal AI knowledge management system. It aggregates personal data like emails and meeting notes and uses AI to make this information readily accessible and actionable.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It offers a practical model for individuals and businesses to leverage AI for enhanced information management and operational efficiency.

## Key properties or components
- Open-sourced
- Personal data aggregation
- AI agent
- Scalable for business use
