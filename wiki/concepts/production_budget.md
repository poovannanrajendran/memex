## Definition
A production budget is a detailed plan outlining the anticipated costs required to complete a project, such as a film, television show, or event. It covers expenses like labour, materials, equipment, and overheads.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The discussion highlights how the $55 million production budget for 'White Horse' was allegedly diverted by Carl Erik Rinsch for personal use, rather than for its intended purpose.

## Key properties or components
- Cost estimation
- Resource allocation
- Project planning
- Financial control
