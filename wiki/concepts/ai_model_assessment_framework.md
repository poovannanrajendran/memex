## Definition
A subjective system used to evaluate the utility, essentiality, and performance of artificial intelligence models or tools.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Helps users prioritise which AI tools to use amidst a rapidly growing market, informing decisions on technology adoption.

## Key properties or components
- Tiered rating system (S, A, B, F)
- Focus on essentiality and daily use
- Consideration of integration capabilities
- Subjective user experience

## Contradictions or debates
The subjectivity of the framework means different users may have vastly different assessments of the same tools.
