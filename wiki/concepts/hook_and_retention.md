## Definition
In video content, a 'hook' is an engaging opening designed to grab the viewer's attention immediately, while 'retention' refers to keeping the viewer engaged throughout the video.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Strong hooks and sustained retention are vital for YouTube's algorithm, leading to higher watch time and better video performance.

## Key properties or components
- Attention-grabbing opening
- Sustained viewer interest
- Reduces audience drop-off
- Key for algorithm favourability
