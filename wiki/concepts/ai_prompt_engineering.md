## Definition
The practice of designing and refining input prompts for artificial intelligence models, particularly large language models and image generation models, to achieve desired outputs. It involves understanding how the AI interprets instructions and strategically structuring queries for optimal results.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for controlling the output of AI generation tools to ensure they meet specific requirements, such as creating realistic AI-generated User Generated Content (UGC). Effective prompt engineering is key to achieving desired aesthetics and avoiding common AI artefacts.

## Key properties or components
- Structured input
- Keyword weighting
- Negative prompting
- Iterative refinement

## Contradictions or debates
The exact interpretation of prompts by different AI models can vary, meaning a prompt that works well for one might not be as effective for another, requiring model-specific tuning.
