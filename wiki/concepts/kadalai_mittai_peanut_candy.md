## Definition
A traditional sweet confection made primarily from roasted peanuts and jaggery (unrefined cane sugar). It is a popular snack in South India, particularly Tamil Nadu.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Represents a traditional food product and a successful small-scale business model in the Erode region. Its production highlights artisanal food crafting.

## Key properties or components
- Made with peanuts and jaggery
- Sweet and crunchy texture
- Typically hand-made
- Popular as a snack and for functions
