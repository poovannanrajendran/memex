## Definition
The process of monitoring and analysing how users interact with a digital product, such as an application or website. It involves collecting data on user actions, navigation paths, and engagement metrics.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for understanding user behaviour, identifying pain points, and optimising the user experience, thereby preventing product failure.

## Key properties or components
- Data collection on user actions
- Analysis of engagement metrics
- Identification of drop-off points
- Informing product improvements

## Contradictions or debates
None.
