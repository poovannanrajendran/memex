## Definition
Engine timing refers to the precise coordination of the ignition of the fuel-air mixture and the opening/closing of engine valves relative to the position of the pistons. Correct timing is essential for optimal engine performance and operation.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Adjusting the engine timing is shown as a critical step in attempting to start the revived MR2 engine.

## Key properties or components
- Coordination of ignition and valve events
- Impacts engine performance and efficiency
- Can be adjusted manually or electronically
