## Definition
A discrepancy between the auditory elements (like voice quality, sound effects) and the visual elements (like video quality, on-screen action) of a piece of media.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This mismatch can break immersion and make content feel inauthentic or jarring to the audience, particularly when a highly polished audio track is paired with low-fidelity visuals.

## Key properties or components
- Inconsistent quality
- Disrupts immersion
- Reduces perceived authenticity
- Can be consciously or unconsciously perceived
