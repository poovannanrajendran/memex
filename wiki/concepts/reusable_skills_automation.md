## Definition
This refers to the process of creating and saving specific sets of actions or commands that can be easily recalled and executed with a single command or trigger.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Automating repetitive tasks saves time and reduces errors, making workflows more efficient. Hermes's ability to turn repeated tasks into one-word commands exemplifies this.

## Key properties or components
- Task identification
- Command creation
- Efficiency
- Repeatability
