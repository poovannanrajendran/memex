## Definition
Weaknesses in a system, application, or process that can be exploited by a threat actor to gain unauthorized access, cause damage, or disrupt operations.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
These are the fundamental flaws that AI is currently exposing, leading to a predicted surge in cyber incidents. Identifying and mitigating them is key to improving security.

## Key properties or components
- Software flaws
- Configuration errors
- Human error
- Outdated systems
