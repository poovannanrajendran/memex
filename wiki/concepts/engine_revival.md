## Definition
Engine revival refers to the process of restoring a dormant or non-functional engine to operational status. This often involves diagnosing and repairing various components that may have degraded due to disuse or age.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for bringing classic cars or vehicles that have been in storage back to life, as demonstrated with the Toyota MR2.

## Key properties or components
- Diagnosis of faults
- Component repair or replacement
- Testing and tuning
- Systematic troubleshooting

## Contradictions or debates
The effectiveness of certain unconventional methods, like the 'hot spark plug' trick, can be debated and may not be a reliable or recommended practice.
