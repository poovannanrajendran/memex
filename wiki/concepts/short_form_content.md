## Definition
Brief video or text-based content designed for quick consumption, typically under 60 seconds, optimised for platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Highly effective for audience engagement, brand awareness, and viral marketing due to its accessibility and shareability on mobile devices.

## Key properties or components
- Concise messaging
- High visual appeal
- Fast-paced editing
- Sound and music integration
- Platform-specific optimisation

## Contradictions or debates
While effective for initial engagement, the brevity can limit in-depth storytelling or complex information delivery.
