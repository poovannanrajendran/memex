## Definition
An artificial intelligence tool that is made available to the public under a licence that permits users to view, use, modify, and distribute the source code. This fosters collaboration and transparency in AI development.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Provides free access to powerful AI capabilities, democratising advanced technology and enabling wider innovation and experimentation without cost barriers.

## Key properties or components
- Free to use
- Source code available
- Permits modification and distribution
- Community-driven development potential

## Contradictions or debates
None.
