## Definition
These are tools that utilise artificial intelligence to assist users in learning, studying, and preparing for examinations. They can process information, generate summaries, create practice questions, and offer personalised feedback.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enhances the effectiveness and efficiency of studying, particularly for complex subjects or large volumes of material, by automating tasks and providing intelligent assistance.

## Key properties or components
- Information synthesis
- Content generation
- Personalisation
- Natural language processing
