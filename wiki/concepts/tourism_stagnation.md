## Definition
A state where the tourism sector of a region experiences a prolonged period of little to no growth or development. This can be due to various factors including lack of investment, poor infrastructure, or ineffective marketing.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video's central argument is that Tamil Nadu's tourism sector is suffering from stagnation, which it attributes to governance and a reliance on PPPs without sufficient government initiative.

## Key properties or components
- Lack of new attractions
- Underdeveloped infrastructure
- Limited visitor growth
- Poor maintenance of existing sites
