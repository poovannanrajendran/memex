## Definition
An optional aircraft system designed to automatically take control of the aircraft, identify a suitable landing site, and perform a safe landing in situations where the pilot is incapacitated or unable to fly.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enhances flight safety by providing a critical fallback system in emergencies, potentially saving lives and the aircraft.

## Key properties or components
- Automated landing capability
- Pilot incapacitation response
- Suitable airport identification
