## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence technologies, such as machine learning and natural language processing, to automate or assist in the creation, design, and development of websites.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
AI can significantly speed up the design process, generate creative options, and optimise user interfaces, making web development more efficient and accessible.

## Key properties or components
- Automation of design tasks
- Content generation
- Personalisation
- Code generation assistance

## Contradictions or debates
Concerns exist regarding the creativity and originality of AI-generated designs, potential job displacement for human designers, and the ethical implications of AI in creative fields.
