## Definition
The series of steps, efforts, and experiences an individual undertakes to achieve their desired level of accomplishment or prosperity.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding the true path to success, as opposed to a perceived easy route, is vital for setting realistic expectations and developing effective strategies. The source warns against being misled by superficial appearances of success.

## Key properties or components
- Effort
- Strategy
- Learning
- Persistence
- Authenticity

## Contradictions or debates
The video suggests that the apparent ease with which some individuals, particularly those with significant wealth or online presence, achieve success can be deceptive, masking the genuine effort and strategic conversations required.
