## Definition
A business model or operational framework where a company can function and grow without any direct human employees or continuous human oversight. All tasks, from management to execution, are handled by automated systems or AI.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept is the core premise of the Paperclip tool, showcasing a future where AI can entirely manage and operate businesses, potentially disrupting traditional employment models.

## Key properties or components
- Full automation
- AI-driven decision-making
- Scalability
- Reduced operational costs

## Contradictions or debates
The practical implementation and long-term viability of a truly 'zero-human' operation, especially in complex or rapidly changing environments, may face challenges related to unforeseen issues, ethical considerations, and the need for human intervention in critical situations.
