## Definition
A music video accompanying a song from a Hindi-language film industry (Bollywood). These videos often feature elaborate dance sequences, vibrant costumes, and narrative elements tied to the film.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Represents a key form of visual media for promoting popular Indian film music, often contributing to cultural trends.

## Key properties or components
- Promotional tool for film songs
- Features actors from the film
- Often includes choreographed dance routines
- Visually dynamic and colourful

## Contradictions or debates
None.
