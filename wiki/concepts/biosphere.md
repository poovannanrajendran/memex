## Definition
The biosphere is the sum of all ecosystems on Earth, encompassing all living organisms and their interactions with the physical environment.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It represents the delicate balance of natural systems that humans rely on for survival, and is shown to be vulnerable to large-scale human interventions like dam construction.

## Key properties or components
- Self-regulating
- Requires balance in atmospheric gases (N2, O2, CO2)
- Supports life through its stable conditions
