## Definition
A mechanism that allows AI models to interface with and utilise external tools or functions in a structured, predictable manner, enabling them to perform specific actions or retrieve information beyond their core generative capabilities.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for FDEs to extend the functionality of AI models, allowing them to interact with enterprise systems like databases and APIs in a controlled and efficient way to solve complex problems.

## Key properties or components
- Enables AI to use external functions
- Provides structured input/output for tools
- Enhances AI capabilities beyond generation
- Supports integration with APIs and databases
