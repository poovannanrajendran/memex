## Definition
A Public-Private Partnership (PPP) is a cooperative arrangement between one or more public sector agencies and one or more private sector entities to deliver a public service or infrastructure project. It aims to leverage the strengths of both sectors.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video argues that the extensive use of PPPs in Tamil Nadu's tourism sector has led to a lack of accountability and effectiveness, suggesting it's a reason for the sector's stagnation.

## Key properties or components
- Shared Risk and Reward
- Private Sector Efficiency
- Public Sector Oversight
- Contractual Agreements
