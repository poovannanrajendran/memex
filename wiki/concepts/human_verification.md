## Definition
Human verification is the process of establishing that an individual interacting with a system is genuinely a human being and not an artificial intelligence or bot. This is becoming increasingly challenging with advanced AI capabilities.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for preventing fraud, scams, and misuse of online services, and for maintaining trust in digital interactions, especially as AI becomes more sophisticated.

## Key properties or components
- Distinguishing humans from AI
- Security against bots
- Preventing identity fraud
- User experience considerations

## Contradictions or debates
The very AI designed to detect bots can also be used to create more sophisticated bots, creating an ongoing arms race.
