## Definition
A novel or unconventional method that utilises Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools to enhance and simplify the process of searching for employment opportunities and making job applications.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
In the context of job searching, AI can automate tedious tasks, identify relevant opportunities more efficiently, and help candidates make more targeted applications, thus potentially increasing success rates.

## Key properties or components
- Leverages AI tools (e.g., language models)
- Aims to improve efficiency in job search
- Focuses on identifying contacts or opportunities
- May involve 'hacks' or non-traditional approaches
