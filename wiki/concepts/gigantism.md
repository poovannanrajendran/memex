## Definition
Gigantism refers to the condition of being abnormally large or oversized. In biology, it is often related to excessive growth hormone production or environmental factors that allow for extreme body size.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding gigantism in animals, particularly in aquatic versus terrestrial environments, helps explain evolutionary strategies and the physical limitations imposed by different habitats.

## Key properties or components
- Extreme body size
- Can be environmentally influenced
- Subject to physical laws (gravity, pressure)
- Can be advantageous or disadvantageous
