## Definition
The sequence of steps and processes involved in creating a video game, from initial concept to final release and post-launch support.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding and optimising the game development workflow is crucial for efficient and effective game creation, especially when integrating AI tools.

## Key properties or components
- Concept and Design
- Asset Creation (3D models, textures, audio)
- Programming and Logic
- Level Design
- Testing and Debugging
- Deployment
