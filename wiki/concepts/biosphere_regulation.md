## Definition
The biosphere is the sum of all ecosystems on Earth, and it has a self-regulating mechanism to maintain balance in its atmospheric composition and other environmental factors. This regulation ensures conditions suitable for life.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding biosphere regulation is crucial for assessing the impact of human activities, such as building dams, which can disrupt natural balances and lead to unintended consequences like climate change.

## Key properties or components
- Atmospheric composition balance (e.g., nitrogen, oxygen, carbon dioxide)
- Self-sustaining and self-correcting mechanisms
- Maintenance of conditions for life
