## Definition
8K resolution refers to a very high-definition video display resolution with a width of approximately 8,000 pixels. It offers significantly more detail and clarity than lower resolutions like 4K or Full HD.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Provides an enhanced viewing experience for visual content, showcasing greater detail and immersion.

## Key properties or components
- Ultra-high definition
- High pixel count (e.g., 7680 × 4320 pixels)
- Enhanced clarity and detail
- Requires advanced display technology
