## Definition
The process by which software developers acquire new knowledge, skills, and understanding related to programming, tools, and best practices. This is essential for career growth and adapting to technological advancements.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The core problem Vibewise addresses is the potential impediment to developer learning caused by current AI coding tools. Ensuring continuous learning is vital for individuals and the industry.

## Key properties or components
- Skill acquisition
- Knowledge retention
- Problem-solving enhancement
- Adaptability to new technologies

## Contradictions or debates
There is a tension between the speed and efficiency offered by AI tools and the depth of learning that often requires more deliberate practice and understanding.
