## Definition
An AI Brain refers to a sophisticated artificial intelligence system designed to replicate or augment human cognitive functions, such as learning, memory, and decision-making. It often involves large datasets and advanced algorithms to process information and generate insights.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Represents a cutting-edge application of AI for personal productivity and organisational efficiency, as demonstrated by Garry Tan's Gbrain.

## Key properties or components
- Data integration
- AI processing
- Information retrieval
- Proactive surfacing of insights
