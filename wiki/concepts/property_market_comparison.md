## Definition
This concept involves analysing and comparing property prices, values, and desirability across different locations. It often focuses on factors like commute times, local amenities, schools, and lifestyle offerings.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video uses this concept to demonstrate that Tonbridge offers a 'sweet spot' in the Kent property market, providing a strong combination of affordability, commute efficiency, and lifestyle amenities compared to its neighbours.

## Key properties or components
- Price comparison
- Commute time analysis
- School catchment value
- Lifestyle assessment
- Investment potential

## Contradictions or debates
Perceived value can be subjective; what one buyer prioritises (e.g., quiet village life) might differ from another's (e.g., vibrant town centre).
