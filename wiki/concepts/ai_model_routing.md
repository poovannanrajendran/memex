## Definition
AI model routing is the process of directing specific tasks or queries to the most suitable AI model based on predefined criteria, such as cost, performance, or task complexity. This ensures optimal resource utilisation.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Key to cost-saving strategies, as it allows simpler tasks to be handled by cheaper models while reserving more powerful, expensive models for complex computations.

## Key properties or components
- Task-to-model matching
- Cost-based selection
- Performance optimisation
- Load balancing
