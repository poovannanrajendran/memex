## Definition
The quality of an artificial intelligence-generated voice in sounding natural and human-like, rather than artificial or robotic. This involves matching vocal inflections, pacing, and emotional tone to context.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for creating engaging and believable AI-generated content, particularly in video production and user-facing applications, to avoid alienating audiences.

## Key properties or components
- Vocal naturalness
- Emotional conveyance
- Pacing and rhythm
- Contextual appropriateness
- Mismatch with visual content

## Contradictions or debates
The common assumption that realism is solely dependent on the sophistication of the voice model itself, ignoring external factors like video quality and audio processing.
