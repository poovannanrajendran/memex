## Definition
A phosphorescent material that absorbs light during the day and emits it during the dark, creating a visible glow. This technology is often incorporated into toys and safety equipment.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
A key feature of the ZipStringer Arachnid, enhancing its playability and visual appeal, with an integrated charging mechanism.

## Key properties or components
- Absorbs light
- Emits light in darkness
- Can be charged by UV light or ambient light
