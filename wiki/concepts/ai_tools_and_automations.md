## Definition
Software or systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, and decision-making (AI Tools), and the use of technology to perform tasks with minimal human intervention (Automations).

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Running these on a dedicated cloud server, even a free one, allows for continuous operation, complex processing, and integration without burdening local hardware. This is crucial for AI development and deployment.

## Key properties or components
- Task automation
- Data processing
- Machine learning model execution
- Continuous operation
