## Definition
AI citations refer to instances where a particular entity, such as a brand or product, is mentioned or referenced within the output generated by an artificial intelligence model. These are akin to references or mentions in traditional search results.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Increasing AI citations is vital for brands aiming to establish authority, credibility, and presence within the AI ecosystem, directly impacting how often they appear in AI-driven interactions.

## Key properties or components
- Mentions in AI responses
- References within LLMs
- Indicators of AI presence
- Query-based relevance

## Contradictions or debates
The process and value of AI citations are still evolving, and there's debate on whether they should be directly manipulated or organically earned.
