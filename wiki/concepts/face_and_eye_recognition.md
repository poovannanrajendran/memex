## Definition
AI functionalities that can identify and recognise human faces within images or videos ('face recognition') and analyse visual characteristics of the eyes, often used for tasks like ranking photos or grouping similar subjects ('eye' as in grouping shots).

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables powerful photo management and organisation tools on local devices, allowing users to easily find and group images of specific individuals without uploading their personal photo libraries.

## Key properties or components
- Facial identification
- Image grouping
- Photo organisation
- Local processing
