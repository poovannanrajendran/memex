## Definition
Specific visual or motion artifacts that betray a video as being synthetically generated by artificial intelligence, rather than being captured by a traditional camera.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding these tells is crucial for creators aiming for realistic AI-generated content and for viewers seeking to discern authentic footage.

## Key properties or components
- Unnatural skin texture (too smooth, no pores)
- Lack of realistic eye reflections or blinking patterns
- Inconsistent or impossible lighting and shadows
- Overly smooth or unrealistic camera movement
- Unnatural rendering of hands or other complex details

## Contradictions or debates
Some specific tells, like those related to hands, may require techniques beyond simple prompt adjustments.
