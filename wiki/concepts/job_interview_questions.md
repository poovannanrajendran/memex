## Definition
Standard inquiries posed by employers during the hiring process to assess a candidate's suitability for a role. These questions typically evaluate skills, experience, personality, and cultural fit.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding common interview questions and preparing thoughtful answers is essential for job seekers to make a positive impression and increase their chances of securing employment.

## Key properties or components
- Tell me about yourself
- What is your greatest strength?
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
- Why should we hire you?
- How do you handle pressure?
- What do you know about our company?
- Do you have any questions for us?
