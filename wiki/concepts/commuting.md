## Definition
The regular journey between one's home and place of work. In the context of property, it often refers to the time and cost involved in travelling to a major employment centre.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
A primary factor for London buyers considering towns like Tonbridge, impacting affordability and lifestyle. Tonbridge's strong commute is presented as a key 'superpower'.

## Key properties or components
- Travel time
- Cost of travel
- Frequency of service
- Train line convergence
- Last stop advantage

## Contradictions or debates
While the commute is fast and frequent, trains can be busy and seats competitive during peak times, with station parking at a premium.
