## Definition
Brand visibility refers to the extent to which a brand is recognised by target consumers and is memorable or easily recalled. In the context of AI, it means being present and discoverable within the responses generated by AI models.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
For brands, increasing visibility within AI chatbots is crucial for reaching potential customers who increasingly rely on these tools for information and recommendations.

## Key properties or components
- Discoverability
- Memorability
- Recognition
- Presence in AI responses

## Contradictions or debates
Some may argue that aggressive self-promotion within AI responses could degrade user experience or be perceived as intrusive.
