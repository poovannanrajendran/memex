## Definition
A concept referring to videos within a specific YouTube niche that significantly outperform average engagement or viewership metrics.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Identifying outliers helps creators understand what resonates most with audiences in their niche, providing a basis for successful content strategy.

## Key properties or components
- Above-average performance
- Niche-specific
- Indicator of audience interest
- Potential for trendsetting
