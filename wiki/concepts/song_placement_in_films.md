## Definition
The strategic positioning of songs within a film's narrative. This includes determining the order, timing, and context of songs to best complement the story and maintain audience interest.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The strategic placement of songs, such as ensuring an opening song sets a tone or a pre-interval song builds momentum, is vital for film pacing and audience reception.

## Key properties or components
- Opening Song
- Mid-film Song
- Pre-Interval Song
- Climax Song
- Narrative Flow
