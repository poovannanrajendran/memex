## Definition
The process of manufacturing goods using human labour and traditional tools, as opposed to automated or mechanised processes.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Cited as the reason for the superior taste and quality of their peanut candy, emphasizing the skill and care involved.

## Key properties or components
- Relies on human skill
- Allows for fine-tuning of processes
- Often slower than automation
- Can lead to unique product characteristics
