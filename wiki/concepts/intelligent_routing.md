## Definition
Intelligent routing in AI refers to the dynamic allocation of tasks to the most appropriate AI model based on factors such as task complexity, cost, and model availability. This ensures optimal performance and cost-efficiency.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Allows platforms like Mixeroute to send simple tasks to cheaper AI models and complex tasks to more powerful ones, significantly reducing overall operational costs.

## Key properties or components
- Task complexity assessment
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Model selection
- Dynamic allocation
