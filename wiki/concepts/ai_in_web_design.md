## Definition
The application of artificial intelligence techniques to automate or assist in various stages of web design and development. This can include tasks like generating layouts, suggesting colour schemes, optimising user interfaces, and even writing code.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
AI has the potential to drastically reduce design and development time, democratise design by making it more accessible, and enhance the efficiency of creative professionals.

## Key properties or components
- Automation of repetitive tasks
- Data-driven design suggestions
- Personalisation
- Predictive analytics

## Contradictions or debates
Concerns exist about AI stifling human creativity and the potential for generic, uninspired designs.
