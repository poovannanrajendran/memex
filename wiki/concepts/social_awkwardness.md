## Definition
Social awkwardness refers to discomfort or ineptitude in social situations, often stemming from a lack of understanding of social cues, norms, or expectations.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Sheldon's social awkwardness is a primary source of comedy and character development in the show, and it's central to how he interacts with Penny and Leonard in this clip.

## Key properties or components
- Difficulty with small talk
- Literal interpretation
- Lack of empathy
- Misreading social cues
- Bluntness

## Contradictions or debates
Some may argue that perceived social awkwardness can sometimes be a deliberate choice or a sign of authenticity rather than a deficit.
