## Definition
Content, such as text, images, videos, and reviews, that is created and published by unpaid contributors, typically users or customers of a platform or brand. It is often seen as more authentic and trustworthy than brand-created content.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video focuses on how to make AI-generated content mimic the authentic appearance of UGC. This is valuable for brands and creators looking to leverage AI for marketing or content creation while maintaining a sense of genuine user engagement.

## Key properties or components
- Authenticity
- User-created
- Varied quality
- Trust factor

## Contradictions or debates
While UGC is often perceived as authentic, the rise of AI-generated content mimicking UGC blurs the lines, potentially reducing the perceived authenticity if not clearly distinguished.
