## Definition
Multi-agent teams involve multiple AI agents collaborating to achieve a common goal. Each agent may have specialised skills or roles, and their interactions are orchestrated to solve complex problems that would be difficult for a single agent.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This advanced concept highlights the potential for Claude Code to coordinate multiple AI agents, enabling sophisticated problem-solving and workflow automation in software development.

## Key properties or components
- Collaboration
- Specialisation
- Coordination
- Shared Goals
