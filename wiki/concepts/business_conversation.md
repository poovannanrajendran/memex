## Definition
A discussion between individuals or parties aimed at achieving a commercial objective, such as making a sale, forming a partnership, or resolving a business-related issue.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Effective business conversations are crucial for building relationships, negotiating deals, and ultimately driving the success of an enterprise. The source highlights that the quality and context of these conversations are paramount.

## Key properties or components
- Purposeful
- Clear communication
- Relationship building
- Goal-oriented

## Contradictions or debates
The source implies that conversations held under the influence of alcohol or in a casual, late-night setting, even if involving potential clients, lack the necessary focus and clarity to be considered effective business conversations.
