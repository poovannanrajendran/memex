## Definition
Specific search functionalities on job platforms designed to help users find employment opportunities that can be performed from any location, often with additional filters to specify requirements like 'remote but must be based in X country/state'.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for individuals seeking flexible work arrangements and for ensuring that remote job listings meet their specific location and logistical needs.

## Key properties or components
- Location-agnostic search
- Specific location requirements
- Work-from-home suitability

## Contradictions or debates
Some 'remote' jobs still have strict geographical constraints, requiring candidates to reside in specific countries or states, which can be misleading without precise filtering.
