## Definition
This concept contrasts historical development paradigms, often driven by socialist ideals and national progress (e.g., 'modern temples'), with contemporary development driven primarily by corporate interests and profit. The latter often prioritises economic gain over environmental and social well-being.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video argues that the shift from nation-building development to profit-driven projects means that constructions like large dams no longer serve the collective good but cater to corporate interests, leading to unsustainable and inequitable outcomes.

## Key properties or components
- Socialism vs. Capitalism
- Corporate interests
- Profit motive
- Sustainability
- Social equity
