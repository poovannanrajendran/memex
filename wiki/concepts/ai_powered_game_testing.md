## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence, such as large language models or reinforcement learning agents, to automate the process of testing video games for bugs and gameplay issues.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept streamlines the QA process by allowing AI to autonomously play the game, identify problems, and even suggest or implement fixes, reducing human effort and time.

## Key properties or components
- Autopilot Gameplay
- Bug Identification
- Automated Bug Fixing
- Performance Analysis

## Contradictions or debates
The effectiveness and comprehensiveness of AI-driven testing compared to human testers can be debated, particularly for nuanced gameplay or emergent bugs.
