## Definition
The process of determining the total expenses involved in producing a 3D printed item, including materials, machine time, labour, and overheads. Accurate calculation is vital for setting competitive prices and ensuring profitability.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for any 3D printing business to understand its financial viability, set appropriate prices, and avoid losses.

## Key properties or components
- Material cost (filament, resin)
- Machine operating cost (electricity, depreciation)
- Print time (labour/machine usage)
- Post-processing time
- Packaging and shipping costs
- Overheads (rent, software, etc.)
- Profit margin
