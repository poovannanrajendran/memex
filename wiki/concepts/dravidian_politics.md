## Definition
Dravidian politics refers to the political movement and ideology originating in the Madras Presidency (now Tamil Nadu and Puducherry) in the early 20th century, emphasising social justice, secularism, regional identity, and opposition to perceived Brahminical dominance and Hindi imposition.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video frames the political landscape of Tamil Nadu primarily as a contest between two major Dravidian parties (DMK and AIADMK), and discusses how NTK interacts within this established political paradigm.

## Key properties or components
- Social justice
- Secularism
- Regionalism
- Anti-caste sentiment
- Tamil identity

## Contradictions or debates
While advocating for Tamil identity, there are internal debates about the extent to which Dravidian parties have truly uplifted all sections of society.
