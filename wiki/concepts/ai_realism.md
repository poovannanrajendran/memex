## Definition
The quality of artificial intelligence-generated content, particularly visual media, that closely resembles real-world objects, scenes, or people. Achieving AI realism often involves replicating subtle details, imperfections, and naturalistic lighting and textures.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for creating AI-generated UGC that is indistinguishable from content created by humans, enhancing its credibility and effectiveness in marketing and communication.

## Key properties or components
- Imperfection replication
- Naturalistic lighting
- Subtle textures
- Asymmetry
- Reduced artefacts

## Contradictions or debates
Over-emphasis on realism can sometimes lead to the 'uncanny valley' effect, where content is close to, but not quite, perfectly realistic, making it feel unsettling.
