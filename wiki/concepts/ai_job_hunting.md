## Definition
The use of artificial intelligence tools and techniques to assist in the process of searching for and applying to jobs. This can include using AI for resume building, job searching, networking, interview preparation, and identifying relevant contacts.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
AI can significantly enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of job hunting by automating repetitive tasks, providing data-driven insights, and enabling more targeted approaches to recruitment.

## Key properties or components
- Automation of tasks
- Data analysis and insights
- Personalised job matching
- Enhanced networking strategies

## Contradictions or debates
Concerns exist about over-reliance on AI leading to a loss of personal touch in applications, potential biases in AI algorithms, and the ethical implications of using AI in hiring processes.
