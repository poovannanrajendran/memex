## Definition
A unique aircraft design where the jet engines are positioned above the wings, rather than the more conventional placement underneath the wings.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This design on the Honda Jet Elite 2 contributes to reduced noise within the cabin and potentially improved aerodynamic efficiency.

## Key properties or components
- Engines mounted above wings
- Reduced cabin noise
- Potential aerodynamic benefits
