## Definition
This term, as used in the transcript, refers to the idea that a political party or candidate treats elections not as a means to serve the public but as a commercial enterprise. It implies profiting from the electoral process through transactions like vote-buying or selling influence.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The transcript alleges that Naam Tamilar Katchi engages in this 'electoral business' by taking money from established parties (DMK and ADMK) in exchange for splitting votes, suggesting their participation in elections is driven by profit rather than public service.

## Key properties or components
- Focus on financial gain over public welfare.
- Transactional relationships with other parties.
- Manipulation of electoral processes for profit.
- Undermining democratic principles.

## Contradictions or debates
Political analysts might debate whether any political party operates entirely without self-interest or financial considerations. The accusation of 'business' implies a level of explicit corruption that might be hard to definitively prove without concrete evidence.
