## Definition
A service model offered by cloud providers that gives users access to a certain amount of computing resources perpetually without charge. These resources typically have usage limits and may not be available in all regions or configurations.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It allows individuals and small businesses to access powerful cloud infrastructure for development, hosting, and experimentation without incurring ongoing costs, making cloud technology more accessible.

## Key properties or components
- Perpetual free access to select resources
- Usage limits (e.g., RAM, storage, data transfer)
- Subject to availability and capacity restrictions
- May require account verification or specific criteria

## Contradictions or debates
The 'free for life' aspect is conditional on Oracle maintaining the program and the user adhering to usage limits; resource availability is not guaranteed.
