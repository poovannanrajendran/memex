## Definition
A foldable phone is a type of mobile device that features a flexible display, allowing it to be folded either inwards or outwards. This design offers a larger screen area when unfolded while maintaining a more compact form factor when closed.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Represents a significant shift in smartphone form factor and design, offering new user experiences and multitasking capabilities, though adoption has been slower than anticipated by some.

## Key properties or components
- Flexible display
- Hinge mechanism
- Compact design
- Large screen when unfolded
- Potential durability concerns

## Contradictions or debates
The reviewer criticises Apple for being slow to adopt foldable technology compared to competitors like Samsung, implying a lack of innovation. The defender counters that Apple prefers to wait until a technology is mature and can be implemented without compromise, avoiding the pitfalls of early-adopter experimental features.
