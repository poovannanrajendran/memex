## Definition
Comedic chemistry refers to the natural and effortless rapport between performers that enhances the humour of their interactions. It makes their dialogue and physical comedy feel spontaneous and believable.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video showcases the comedic chemistry between Kevin Hart and Sofia Vergara, making their discussion engaging and entertaining, which is a key aspect of their public personas.

## Key properties or components
- Natural rapport
- Enhanced humour
- Spontaneity
- Believability
