## Definition
A formal examination or review of an organisation or its operations by government officials or agencies. These are typically conducted to ensure compliance with laws and regulations or to investigate specific issues.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The source describes a minister conducting an inspection shortly after appointment, a standard procedure. However, the subsequent disappearance of hard disks during this inspection raises concerns about the integrity and purpose of such reviews.

## Key properties or components
- Official oversight
- Compliance checks
- Investigation
