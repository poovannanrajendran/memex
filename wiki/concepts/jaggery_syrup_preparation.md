## Definition
The process of heating and dissolving jaggery (unrefined cane sugar) to create a syrup of a specific consistency, which is crucial for making sweets like peanut candy. The syrup must reach a particular 'pazam' or stage to ensure the candy has the right texture.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The correct preparation of jaggery syrup is vital for the texture and quality of the final product. An incorrect consistency can result in the candy being too hard, too soft, or crumbling.

## Key properties or components
- Heating jaggery
- Achieving specific viscosity/stage ('pazam')
- Requires careful monitoring
- Manual control is often preferred for precision
