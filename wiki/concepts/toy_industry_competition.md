## Definition
This concept refers to the challenges faced by physical toy manufacturers in a market increasingly dominated by digital entertainment and screen-based activities. It highlights the struggle to capture children's attention and playtime.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Explains the inventor's motivation for seeking help to promote physical toys and highlights the broader market context for products like the ZipStringer Arachnid.

## Key properties or components
- Digital vs. physical play
- Screen time influence
- Market share challenges
- Marketing hurdles
