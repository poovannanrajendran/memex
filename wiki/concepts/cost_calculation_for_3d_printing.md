## Definition
The process of determining all expenses associated with producing a 3D printed item, including materials, energy, machine time, labour, and overheads. This is crucial for setting a profitable selling price.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for any 3D printing business to understand its profitability and to price products competitively yet sustainably. Without accurate cost calculation, businesses risk underpricing and incurring losses.

## Key properties or components
- Material cost (filament weight, type)
- Energy cost (electricity consumption)
- Machine time (print duration)
- Labour cost (design, post-processing, packing)
- Overheads (machine maintenance, software, workspace)
- Packaging and shipping costs
