## Definition
The 'oneshot rate' is a metric that measures the frequency with which an AI model successfully completes a given task on its very first attempt, without requiring further iteration or correction.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This metric indicates the efficiency and accuracy of an AI model, providing insights into how reliably it can perform tasks, such as bug fixes or code generation, from the outset.

## Key properties or components
- First-attempt success
- Task completion efficiency
- AI accuracy
- Reduced iteration
