## Definition
Artificial intelligence models that are specifically designed to be compact in size and computationally efficient. They are often trained for specific, narrow tasks to reduce resource requirements.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for enabling AI to run on resource-constrained devices like smartphones, making advanced AI functionalities more accessible and practical for widespread, offline use.

## Key properties or components
- Small file size
- Low computational requirements
- Task-specific
- Efficient

## Contradictions or debates
May lack the generalisation capabilities and performance of larger, more complex AI models.
