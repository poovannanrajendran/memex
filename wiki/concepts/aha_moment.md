## Definition
The point in time when a user first experiences the core value or benefit of a product. It's the moment of realisation that the product is useful and solves a problem for them.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Critical for user retention and adoption; without it, users are unlikely to see the long-term value and may abandon the product.

## Key properties or components
- Discovery of core value
- Moment of user realisation
- Drives engagement and retention
- Needs to be reached quickly

## Contradictions or debates
Some argue that defining a single 'aha moment' can be overly simplistic, as value can be realised incrementally or through various features.
