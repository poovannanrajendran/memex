## Definition
Options trading involves contracts that give the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying asset at a specified price on or before a certain date.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It is specifically mentioned as a type of trading portfolio that the AI might be applied to, indicating its complexity and potential for AI involvement.

## Key properties or components
- Leverage
- Defined risk (for buyers)
- Complex strategies
- Expiration dates
