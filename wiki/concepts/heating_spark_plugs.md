## Definition
A non-standard and potentially damaging technique where spark plugs are heated to aid in starting a stubborn engine. The theory is that heat may help ignite the fuel-air mixture in the cylinder.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This unconventional method is the surprise 'trick' that ultimately allows the MR2 engine to run after all other attempts failed.

## Key properties or components
- Unconventional repair method
- Involves applying heat to spark plugs
- Can be risky and potentially damage components
- May temporarily aid in ignition

## Contradictions or debates
This method directly contradicts standard automotive repair practices, as excessive heat can damage spark plugs and potentially other engine components. It is generally not recommended.
