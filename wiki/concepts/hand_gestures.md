## Definition
Hand gestures are non-verbal cues communicated through the movement of hands and arms. In the context of XR and AR/VR, hand gestures are used as an input method to interact with virtual environments and objects.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enables intuitive and natural interaction with the Samsung Galaxy XR, allowing users to manipulate virtual objects and navigate interfaces without physical controllers.

## Key properties or components
- Natural user input
- Requires tracking technology
- Allows manipulation of virtual elements
