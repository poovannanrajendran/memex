## Definition
Actor politics refers to the phenomenon where popular film actors transition into the political arena, leveraging their fame and fan base to gain political influence or win elections. This trend is particularly notable in Indian cinema, especially in Tamil Nadu.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept is highly relevant due to the prominent use of 'Thalapathy Vijay' hashtags, indicating the video is likely connected to his political activities or the broader trend of actors entering politics.

## Key properties or components
- Leveraging star power
- Fan base mobilisation
- Appeal beyond traditional party structures
- Potential for populist messaging

## Contradictions or debates
Critics argue that fame does not necessarily translate to good governance or political acumen, and that actors may lack the necessary understanding of complex policy issues.
