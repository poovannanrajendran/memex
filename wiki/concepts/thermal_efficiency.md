## Definition
Thermal efficiency in electronic devices refers to how effectively heat generated during operation is managed and dissipated. Improved thermal efficiency prevents overheating, allows components to perform at higher capacities for longer periods, and can contribute to device longevity.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video argues that enhanced thermal management, enabled by the A17 Pro chip and design improvements, is critical for unlocking sustained performance, especially in demanding tasks like gaming and AI processing.

## Key properties or components
- Heat dissipation
- Component longevity
- Sustained performance
- Overheating prevention

## Contradictions or debates
One speaker initially dismisses the importance of thermal efficiency, stating they've never experienced thermal issues, suggesting it's an unnecessary focus for the average user.
