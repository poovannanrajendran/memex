## Definition
Speculative investments are financial ventures with a high risk of loss, undertaken in the hope of substantial returns. These often involve volatile assets like stock options or cryptocurrencies, where the future value is uncertain.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This concept is central to how Carl Erik Rinsch allegedly misused Netflix's funds, attempting to generate quick profits through high-risk trades.

## Key properties or components
- High risk of loss
- Potential for high returns
- Uncertain future value
- Often involves derivatives or new asset classes
