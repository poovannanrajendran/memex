## Definition
The concept of deliberately waiting for the opportune moment to act rather than rushing into decisions or actions. It involves strategic waiting, observation, and timing.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Central to understanding Aaron Burr's character and his approach to ambition and conflict with Hamilton. It informs his philosophical outlook and decision-making.

## Key properties or components
- Observation
- Timing
- Strategic delay
- Foresight
- Controlled action

## Contradictions or debates
Can be seen as passive or indecisive if not balanced with eventual action; risks missing opportunities if timing is misjudged.
