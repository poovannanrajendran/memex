## Definition
Development that prioritises economic gain and profit for corporations or a select few, often at the expense of environmental sustainability and social equity.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video critiques this model of development, arguing that large infrastructure projects are no longer driven by a vision for societal progress but by corporate interests, benefiting only a minority.

## Key properties or components
- Focus on return on investment
- Potential for environmental degradation
- Social inequality

## Contradictions or debates
Often contrasted with sustainable development or development for the common good.
