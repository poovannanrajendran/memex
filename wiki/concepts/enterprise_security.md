## Definition
Enterprise Security, in the context of AI integration, pertains to the specific measures and practices required to deploy and manage AI models securely within large organisations. This includes protecting sensitive data and ensuring compliance with regulations.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
A core skill for FDEs, enabling them to deploy AI solutions within secure private cloud environments (VPCs), enforce granular access controls, and adhere to data residency and compliance mandates critical for enterprise adoption.

## Key properties or components
- Private Cloud (VPC) deployment
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Data Residency Compliance
- Network Security
