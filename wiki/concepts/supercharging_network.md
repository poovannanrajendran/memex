## Definition
The Supercharging network is a proprietary, high-speed charging infrastructure developed and operated by Tesla for its electric vehicles. It allows Tesla owners to rapidly recharge their car batteries during long journeys.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Access to a reliable and widespread fast-charging network is critical for the usability and practicality of electric vehicles, especially for long-distance travel. Tesla's network is a significant competitive advantage for the brand, and denial of access severely hampers a vehicle's utility.

## Key properties or components
- High-speed charging
- Proprietary to Tesla vehicles (primarily)
- Extensive global coverage
- Key enabler for long-distance EV travel
- Can be restricted by vehicle status (e.g., salvage)
