## Definition
This is a strategy where a system assesses the difficulty or complexity of a given task and directs it to the most appropriate computational resource or AI model. Simpler tasks go to less powerful/cheaper resources, while complex tasks are handled by more advanced ones.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This is a key optimisation feature of Rufflow, allowing for significant cost savings (token usage) and efficient utilisation of AI models by matching task demands to resource capabilities.

## Key properties or components
- Task assessment
- Resource allocation
- Cost optimisation
- Efficiency
