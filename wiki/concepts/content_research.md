## Definition
Content research involves the systematic investigation and analysis of information for the purpose of creating or understanding content. This can include identifying trending topics, audience interests, and competitive analysis.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Effective content research, aided by AI tools like Hermes, allows for the discovery of unique angles and timely information, enhancing content quality and relevance.

## Key properties or components
- Information gathering
- Trend identification
- Topic discovery
- Audience analysis
