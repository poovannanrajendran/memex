## Definition
The background information, state, and relevant data that an AI agent uses to understand its current situation and make informed decisions.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Proper context management is vital as it directly impacts an agent's ability to perform tasks accurately and relevantly; without it, agents can fail or produce nonsensical outputs.

## Key properties or components
- Relevant data
- State tracking
- Task relevance
- Environmental awareness
