## Definition
A standard protocol designed to facilitate the secure and structured interaction between AI models and external data sources or systems, ensuring that the model receives relevant information within its operational context.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Key to enabling FDEs to build reliable AI applications by providing a standardised way for models to access and utilise information from diverse enterprise data stores and services.

## Key properties or components
- Facilitates secure model interaction
- Defines structured data exchange
- Enables context-aware AI operations
- Aiding integration with legacy systems
