## Definition
The process of coordinating and managing multiple AI agents or AI agent tasks to achieve a larger, more complex goal.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for building sophisticated AI systems where individual agents perform specific functions, and orchestration ensures they work together seamlessly and efficiently.

## Key properties or components
- Task delegation
- Workflow management
- Inter-agent communication
- Resource allocation
