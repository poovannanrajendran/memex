## Definition
The process of using artificial intelligence models to create images that mimic the aesthetic styles of past eras, such as the 1950s, 1980s, or early 2000s.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Allows users to easily recreate nostalgic visual styles for content creation, marketing, or personal expression without needing traditional design skills.

## Key properties or components
- Mimics historical aesthetics
- Utilises AI models
- Often prompt-driven
