## Definition
The process of identifying and obscuring sensitive or private information within data, often by replacing it with generic placeholders. This is crucial for protecting personal details before data is shared or processed by external systems.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Protects sensitive information like names, addresses, or financial details, especially when using cloud-based AI services, thereby enhancing privacy and security.

## Key properties or components
- Sensitive data identification
- Information masking
- Placeholder replacement
- Privacy preservation
