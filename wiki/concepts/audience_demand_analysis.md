## Definition
The process of systematically reviewing audience feedback, comments, and engagement data to understand what content viewers are actively seeking or requesting.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Creating content that aligns with audience demand increases the likelihood of engagement, satisfaction, and channel growth.

## Key properties or components
- Comment mining
- Engagement metric analysis
- Identifies viewer needs
- Informs content strategy
