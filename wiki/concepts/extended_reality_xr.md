## Definition
Extended Reality (XR) is an umbrella term that encompasses virtual reality (VR), augmented reality (AR), and mixed reality (MR). It refers to all computer-generated environments and interactions that merge the physical and digital worlds.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding XR is crucial for grasping the capabilities and potential applications of devices like the Samsung Galaxy XR, which aims to provide immersive digital experiences.

## Key properties or components
- Immersive digital environments
- Merging of physical and digital worlds
- Includes VR, AR, and MR
