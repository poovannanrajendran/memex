## Definition
The process of evaluating and ordering different online platforms or websites used for job searching based on criteria such as effectiveness, user experience, and success rate in securing employment.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Helps job seekers identify the most effective tools for their job search, saving time and increasing the likelihood of securing interviews and offers.

## Key properties or components
- Evaluation criteria (e.g., application success, user interface, features)
- Comparative analysis
- Prioritisation of platforms

## Contradictions or debates
Rankings can be subjective and depend on individual needs, industry, and career level. What works for one person might not work for another.
