## Definition
The process of identifying and obtaining contact information for Human Resources professionals within a company. This is often done to facilitate direct communication regarding job applications or career opportunities.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Directly contacting HR can help job seekers tailor their applications, inquire about specific roles, and make their candidacy stand out from generic applications submitted through online portals.

## Key properties or components
- Identifying relevant individuals
- Obtaining contact details (email, LinkedIn)
- Targeted communication
- Networking within HR departments

## Contradictions or debates
Some job seekers may face difficulties in finding direct HR contacts, and companies may have policies against direct unsolicited contact, preferring applicants to go through official channels.
