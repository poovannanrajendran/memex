## Definition
An unconventional method involving heating spark plugs to a high temperature, often using flammable liquids like brake cleaner, to temporarily ignite the fuel-air mixture and start a stubborn engine.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This technique is presented as a last resort or experimental fix for engines that refuse to start through conventional means. It allowed the MR2 engine to run for the first time in years.

## Key properties or components
- Use of flammable liquids (e.g., brake parts cleaner) for ignition
- Heating the spark plugs to extreme temperatures
- Temporary solution for starting
- Potential risk of damage to spark plugs and engine components

## Contradictions or debates
This method is highly unconventional and carries significant risks, being explicitly warned against by the presenter for DIY attempts.
