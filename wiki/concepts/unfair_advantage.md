## Definition
A factor or strategy that provides a significant competitive edge, often through non-obvious means or by leveraging personal attributes or approaches that most others do not employ. In the job market, this can be extra effort or unique skills.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
In a competitive job market, identifying and utilising 'unfair advantages' like demonstrating exceptional effort or unique problem-solving skills can dramatically improve a candidate's chances of success.

## Key properties or components
- Exceeding expectations
- Unique skills/knowledge
- Proactive initiative
- Diligence

## Contradictions or debates
The term 'unfair' can be subjective; what one person sees as an advantage, another might see as standard practice or simply hard work.
