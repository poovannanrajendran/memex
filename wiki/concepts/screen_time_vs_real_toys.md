## Definition
The ongoing debate and competition between digital entertainment (screens, video games) and physical, tangible toys for children's attention and playtime.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The creator identifies this as a major challenge, seeking to encourage children to engage with physical toys like the ZipStringer Arachnid amidst the dominance of digital media.

## Key properties or components
- Digital entertainment
- Physical play
- Child development
- Market competition
