## Definition
Scientific agent skills are a set of specialised capabilities that enable an AI agent to perform research-oriented tasks, such as literature review, data analysis, and hypothesis generation, akin to a human research assistant.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Transforms general-purpose AI agents into powerful tools for scientific discovery and innovation, accelerating the pace of research.

## Key properties or components
- Research assistance
- Data interpretation
- Literature synthesis
