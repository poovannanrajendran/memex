## Definition
A 'Gujju business brain' is a colloquial term referring to the perceived sharp, entrepreneurial, and often shrewd business acumen associated with individuals from the Gujarati community in India. It implies a natural talent for commerce, negotiation, and strategic thinking.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Used metaphorically to describe the type of advanced business intelligence that Garry Tan's AI system aims to emulate or enhance.

## Key properties or components
- Entrepreneurial
- Shrewd
- Negotiation skills
- Strategic thinking

## Contradictions or debates
This is a colloquialism and potentially a stereotype, not a formal business concept.
