## Definition
A type of information filtering system that seeks to predict the 'rating' or 'preference' a user would give to an item. These systems are used to suggest relevant content, products, or services to users.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Crucial for filtering vast amounts of online content, like videos, to present users with relevant and engaging material, which is the core function of Hackertube.org.

## Key properties or components
- Content-based filtering
- Collaborative filtering
- Hybrid approaches
- User preference prediction
