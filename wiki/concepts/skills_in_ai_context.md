## Definition
In the context of AI agents, 'skills' refer to specific capabilities or functions that the agent can perform. These can be learned, programmed, or refined over time.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The ability to build and reuse skills is key to the efficiency and adaptability of AI agents, allowing for customisation and automation of complex workflows.

## Key properties or components
- Reusability
- Modularity
- Programmability
- Learning/Refinement
