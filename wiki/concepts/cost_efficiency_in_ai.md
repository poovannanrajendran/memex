## Definition
Cost efficiency in AI refers to optimising the use of computational resources and API calls to achieve desired outcomes at the lowest possible expense. This often involves strategic use of different AI models or tiers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Rufflow demonstrates significant cost savings (75% cheaper) by intelligently routing tasks to free tiers and only using expensive models when absolutely necessary, addressing a major concern for AI users.

## Key properties or components
- Optimised resource allocation
- Tiered model usage
- API cost management
- Budget control
- Maximising value
