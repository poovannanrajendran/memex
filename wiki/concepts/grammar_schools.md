## Definition
State-funded schools that admit pupils based on academic ability, typically assessed through an examination (the 11-plus). They are known for their high academic standards and selective intake.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The presence of 'super-selective' grammar schools like The Judd School and Tonbridge Grammar School is a major draw for families, positioning Tonbridge as an 'Education Capital'.

## Key properties or components
- Academic selection
- High performance rankings
- Limited places
- Catchment areas/Distance as factor

## Contradictions or debates
Living in Tonbridge does not guarantee a place; admission is primarily based on exam scores, with distance being a secondary factor.
