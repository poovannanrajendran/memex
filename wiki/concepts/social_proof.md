## Definition
Social proof is a psychological and social phenomenon where people copy the actions of others in an attempt to undertake behaviour in situations uncertain or ambiguous. In business, it refers to the influence of customers' decisions based on the actions and opinions of others.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Leveraging social proof, such as testimonials, can significantly influence potential customers' purchasing decisions and build trust in a brand.

## Key properties or components
- Influence of others
- Builds trust
- Reduces uncertainty
- Testimonials
- Reviews
- Case studies
