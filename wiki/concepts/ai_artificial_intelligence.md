## Definition
The simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, and self-correction.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Acts as an accelerant for cybersecurity threats by exposing existing software vulnerabilities and potentially creating new attack methods, while also being a tool for defence.

## Key properties or components
- Machine learning
- Natural language processing
- Automation
- Pattern recognition
