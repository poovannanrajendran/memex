## Definition
Government corruption is the abuse of entrusted power for private gain. This can include bribery, embezzlement, and the manipulation of information or processes to conceal wrongdoing.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This video touches upon potential government corruption by suggesting the concealment of facts related to missing data within a government department, impacting public trust.

## Key properties or components
- Abuse of power
- Lack of transparency
- Intentional misinformation
- Impact on public services
