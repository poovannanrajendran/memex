## Definition
A three-layer framework proposed by Andrej Karpathy for effectively interacting with and leveraging AI models, focusing on precise specification, rigorous verification, and a structured, persistent environment.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Aims to significantly improve the speed, quality, and reliability of AI-assisted development and tasks.

## Key properties or components
- Layer 1: The Spec (Defining goals, requirements, and process)
- Layer 2: The Verifier (Establishing evaluation criteria and checks)
- Layer 3: The Environment (Creating persistent workspaces and guardrails)
