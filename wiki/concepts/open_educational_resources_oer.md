## Definition
Educational materials that are free to use, adapt, and share. This includes textbooks, course materials, videos, and assessments that are licensed in a way that permits open access and modification.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Increases accessibility and affordability of educational content, allowing for customisation to specific learning needs and contexts.

## Key properties or components
- Free access
- Adaptability
- Shareability
- Open licensing

## Contradictions or debates
None.
