## Definition
Elastin-like polypeptides are synthetic biopolymers designed to mimic the properties of elastin, a natural protein that provides elasticity to tissues. They can be engineered to respond to environmental stimuli and self-assemble into specific structures.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
ELPs are used here as a biocompatible scaffold that can deliver minerals and rebuild tooth enamel, demonstrating their potential in regenerative medicine and biomaterials.

## Key properties or components
- Synthetic biopolymers
- Mimic elastin properties
- Responsive to stimuli
- Self-assembly capabilities
