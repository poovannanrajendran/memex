## Definition
High-Velocity Prototyping is an agile approach to quickly developing functional prototypes from initial, often ambiguous, business requirements. The focus is on rapid iteration and delivering production-ready architecture in a compressed timeframe.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Allows FDEs to rapidly translate complex and unclear enterprise problems into working solutions, significantly speeding up the process from concept to deployable system, contrasting with longer traditional development cycles.

## Key properties or components
- Rapid iteration
- Translating ambiguity
- Functional architecture
- Production-ready output
- Short development cycles

## Contradictions or debates
None.
