## Definition
A distribution strategy outlines the methods and channels used to consistently acquire new users or customers for a product or application. It focuses on repeatable processes for reaching the target audience.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Vital for sustained growth and survival; without a reliable way to get users, even a good product will eventually fail due to lack of engagement and revenue.

## Key properties or components
- Focuses on acquisition
- Aims for consistency
- Requires repeatable processes
- Encompasses marketing and outreach
