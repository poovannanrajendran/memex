## Definition
A system of numerical representation using only two symbols, typically 0 and 1. It is the fundamental language of digital computers.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Forms the basis of the power symbol's design and the operation of all modern electronic devices.

## Key properties or components
- Uses only two digits: 0 and 1.
- Core of digital computing.
- Represents 'off' (0) and 'on' (1).
