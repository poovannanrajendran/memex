## Definition
AI token usage refers to the consumption of 'tokens', which are fundamental units of text or data processed by artificial intelligence models. Each interaction, whether input or output, consumes a certain number of tokens, directly impacting the cost and performance of AI services.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Understanding token usage is crucial for managing costs and optimising the efficiency of AI tools like Claude, especially for developers who rely on these services for coding tasks.

## Key properties or components
- Cost implication
- Performance metric
- Resource consumption
- Input/Output dependency
