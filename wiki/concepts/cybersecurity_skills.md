## Definition
Cybersecurity skills for AI agents involve equipping them with the knowledge and capabilities to understand, detect, and respond to security threats, or to perform security-related tasks like code analysis.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Enhances the safety and reliability of AI systems, allowing them to operate securely or to actively contribute to the security posture of other systems.

## Key properties or components
- Threat detection
- Vulnerability assessment
- Secure coding practices
