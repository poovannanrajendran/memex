## Definition
Predefined rules, constraints, or safety mechanisms implemented to guide an AI's behaviour and prevent undesirable or harmful actions. They ensure AI operates within ethical, legal, and functional boundaries.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for controlling AI behaviour, preventing errors, ensuring safety, and enforcing critical operational rules that AI might otherwise bypass if only given softer instructions.

## Key properties or components
- Rule-based constraints
- Safety mechanisms
- Behavioural limits
- Enforcement at tool/system level

## Contradictions or debates
Overly strict guardrails can stifle AI creativity and utility; finding the right balance between control and flexibility is key. Some argue that the most robust guardrails need to be implemented at the system or tool level, not just within prompts.
