## Definition
The use of symbols to represent ideas or qualities. In technology, symbols are crucial for intuitive user interfaces.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
It helps in creating easily understandable and globally recognised icons for complex functions, reducing the need for text and language barriers.

## Key properties or components
- Represents abstract concepts
- Aids in communication
- Can have cultural variations
- Often relies on visual metaphors

## Contradictions or debates
The meaning of symbols can sometimes be ambiguous or evolve over time, leading to potential misinterpretations.
