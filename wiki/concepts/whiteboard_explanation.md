## Definition
A whiteboard explanation is a method of conveying complex information, ideas, or processes by drawing diagrams, writing key points, and illustrating concepts on a physical or digital whiteboard.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
This technique is often used in technical interviews and collaborative settings to visually structure thought processes and ensure clarity and comprehension of intricate subjects like AI agent functionality.

## Key properties or components
- Visual communication
- Conceptual clarity
- Structured thinking
- Interactive learning
- Problem-solving illustration
