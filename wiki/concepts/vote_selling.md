## Definition
Vote selling is the practice where voters or political parties accept money or other inducements in exchange for their votes, undermining the democratic principle of free and fair elections.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
The video explicitly accuses NTK of engaging in vote selling, alleging they receive payments from major parties to split votes, which is presented as their primary motivation for contesting elections.

## Key properties or components
- Bribery
- Corruption
- Undermining democracy
- Financial motivation for political participation
