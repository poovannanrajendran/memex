## Definition
The challenge and development of technologies or methods designed to reliably distinguish between human users and artificial intelligence systems, particularly in online interactions.

## Why it matters (in Poovi's context)
Essential for preventing sophisticated AI-driven scams, ensuring the integrity of online platforms, and protecting vulnerable users who may be targeted by automated fraudulent activities.

## Key properties or components
- Biometric verification
- Behavioural analysis
- CAPTCHA alternatives
- Digital identity

## Contradictions or debates
The arms race between AI generation capabilities and AI detection methods means no system is perfectly foolproof.
