## Summary
This YouTube video discusses the arrest of Prakash, a journalist from Nakkheeran magazine, for allegedly spreading defamatory remarks about Malathi, a journalist who previously worked for Savukku Media. Malathi reportedly filed a complaint after Prakash falsely accused her of having AIDS on a YouTube channel. The video highlights the distress such accusations can cause, especially to women in public life, and criticises the delay in action and the societal acceptance of such behaviour. Prakash was arrested by Chennai police and sent to judicial custody.

## Key claims
- Journalist Malathi faced defamation and false cases due to her work with Savukku Media.
- A journalist from Nakkheeran, Prakash, made defamatory statements about Malathi, falsely claiming she had AIDS.
- Such defamatory statements can cause significant mental distress and social damage to the victim.
- Despite a complaint, action was allegedly delayed.
- Prakash was arrested by the Chennai City Police based on Malathi's complaint and sent to judicial custody.
