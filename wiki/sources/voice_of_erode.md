## Summary
This video showcases the traditional production of peanut candy (kadalai mittai) in Erode, Tamil Nadu. The speaker, who took over the family business from his father, details the entire process from sourcing roasted peanuts and jaggery to the meticulous steps of preparing the syrup, setting the candy, and cutting it. The business primarily operates on a wholesale model, supplying to various towns and also catering to functions like weddings and baby showers.

## Key claims
- The business has been operating for 30 years, initially run by the father and now by the son.
- They produce peanut candy, elumittai, idichu mittai, and ginger candy.
- Peanuts are sourced from Karur and roasted locally. Jaggery is sourced from Akkarai.
- The entire production process, except for packaging, is done manually to ensure taste and quality.
- The business operates on a wholesale model, serving areas like Erode, Namakkal, Tiruchengode, and Vellore.
- Candy is available in various price points: ₹5, ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, and ₹60.
- They also supply candies for special occasions like weddings and valaikappu (baby shower ceremonies).
- The production capacity is around 210-220 kg per day.
- The location of the company is Kodumudi, Erode district.
