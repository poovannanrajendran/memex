## Summary
This video showcases the traditional production of peanut candy (kadalai mittai) by a family in Erode, Tamil Nadu. The business, which has been running for 30 years, is now managed by the second generation. They produce four types of sweets: peanut candy, elumittai, idicha mittai, and ginger candy. The process involves roasting peanuts, breaking them, preparing jaggery syrup, and manually shaping and cutting the candy. They emphasize the manual nature of the production, particularly the syrup consistency, as key to the taste and quality. The business operates on a wholesale basis and supplies to various districts, with special orders for functions and events.
