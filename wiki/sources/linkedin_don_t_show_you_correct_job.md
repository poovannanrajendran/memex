## Summary
This video suggests that LinkedIn is not the most effective platform for job searching, as many roles are not listed there, leading to high competition. It proposes two alternative strategies: first, using Google to search for jobs directly on company hiring platforms like Ashby, Greenhouse, or Lever, which tend to receive fewer applications. Second, for startups without open roles, it recommends reaching out directly to founders with a researched pitch, outlining how the applicant can add value, rather than just sending a CV.

## Key claims
- Most jobs are not listed on LinkedIn.
- Applying only on LinkedIn results in competing with a large number of applicants.
- Searching for jobs directly on hiring platforms (e.g., site:jobs.ashbyhq.com) yields better results.
- Startups without advertised roles may have opportunities if approached directly.
- Pitching a specific role and demonstrating how you can add value is more effective than just sending a resume.
- Effort is a significant unfair advantage in the job market.
