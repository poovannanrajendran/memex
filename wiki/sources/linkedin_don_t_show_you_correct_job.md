## Summary
This video suggests that relying solely on LinkedIn for job applications is inefficient due to high competition. It recommends two alternative strategies: firstly, using Google to search for jobs directly on company hiring platforms like AshbyHQ.com, Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday, which often receive fewer applicants. Secondly, for startups without open roles, it advises identifying the founder, researching the company's domain and mission, and then pitching a specific role by highlighting how you can add value, rather than just sending a CV.

## Key claims
- Most jobs are not listed on LinkedIn.
- Applying on LinkedIn leads to competing with a large number of candidates.
- Searching for jobs on specific hiring platforms (e.g., AshbyHQ.com, Greenhouse) can lead to fewer applicants.
- Pitching a role to a startup founder with a clear value proposition is more effective than a generic application.
- Effort and a proactive approach are significant advantages in the job market.
