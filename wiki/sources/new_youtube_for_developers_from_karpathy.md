## Summary
Andrej Karpathy has created a new YouTube-like platform called Hackertube.org, built over a single weekend, to address his dissatisfaction with YouTube's recommendation algorithms. He curated a list of top developer tutorial videos from Hacker News comments to build his own recommendation system, aiming to help developers become top 1% in their field.

## Key claims
- Andrej Karpathy built Hackertube.org in one weekend.
- Hackertube.org is a recommendation system for developer videos.
- The system was created due to dissatisfaction with existing YouTube recommendations.
- It curates videos based on recommendations from Hacker News.
- The platform aims to help developers improve their skills and reach the top 1%.
