## Summary
This video explains how to improve AI-generated User Generated Content (UGC) by structuring prompts more effectively. Instead of writing prompts as a paragraph, the presenter advises breaking them down into distinct blocks with headings. Each block should cover specific aspects like the subject, visual style, facial details, camera work, and actions, with a crucial negative prompt section to avoid common AI pitfalls like perfect symmetry and cinematic colour grading.

## Key claims
- AI models process prompts as a weighted bag of phrases, not in a narrative flow.
- Structuring prompts in blocks with headings yields better results than paragraph-style prompts.
- Specific blocks should detail subject, visual style, skin/face, camera, and actions.
- Negative prompts are essential to prevent AI from creating overly perfect or stylised outputs.
- Avoiding perfect symmetry and cinematic colour grading (teal and orange) is key to making AI UGC look more authentic.
