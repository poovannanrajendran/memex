## Summary
This video explains a more effective method for crafting AI prompts to generate realistic User Generated Content (UGC). Instead of writing prompts as a continuous paragraph, it suggests structuring them into distinct blocks with headings. Each block focuses on specific elements like the subject's appearance, visual style, facial details, camera work, and actions. Crucially, it advises using a negative prompt block to exclude undesirable elements like perfect symmetry and overly cinematic colour grading, which AI models often default to.

## Key claims
- AI models process prompts as a weighted bag of phrases, not like human readers.
- Structuring prompts into blocks with headings yields better results than paragraph-style prompts.
- Key elements to include in prompt blocks are subject identity, visual style, skin/face details, camera type, and physical actions.
- Negative prompts are essential for avoiding common AI generation flaws such as perfect symmetry and cinematic colour grading.
- Realism in AI UGC is enhanced by specifying imperfections like visible pores and slight asymmetry.
