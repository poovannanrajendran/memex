## Summary
This YouTube video discusses the environmental impact of large dams, questioning their necessity and highlighting issues caused by them. It touches upon the Earth's natural balance, climate change, and how human interventions like dam construction disrupt ecosystems. The video contrasts past justifications for large infrastructure projects, seen as 'modern temples' by leaders like Nehru, with current practices driven by corporate interests and profit, suggesting that such development primarily benefits a small percentage of the population.

## Key claims
- Large dams disrupt natural ecosystems and Earth's balance, potentially leading to severe problems.
- The Earth's biosphere is a self-regulating system with specific ratios of gases like nitrogen and oxygen, and altering these ratios can have significant consequences (e.g., increased heat due to higher carbon dioxide).
- Evolutionary adaptation, as described by Darwin, means only species that can adapt to new environments survive.
- Many developed countries are dismantling dams, suggesting they are becoming obsolete due to the problems they create.
- While small check dams might be permissible, mega-dams pose significant environmental threats.
- Past large infrastructure projects were driven by socialist ideals and a vision for progress, whereas current projects are often motivated by profit and corporate interests.
- The current model of development and construction primarily benefits a small percentage of the population, not the entire region or country.
