## Summary
The HondaJet Elite II is a compact private jet capable of carrying up to seven passengers and reaching altitudes of 43,000 ft. It features a unique over-the-wing engine mount design and offers an optional emergency auto-land system. The jet has a range of 2,865 km and a list price of approximately $6.95 million.
