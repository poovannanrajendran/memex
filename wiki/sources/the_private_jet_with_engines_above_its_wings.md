## Summary
The Honda Jet Elite 2 is a compact private jet capable of carrying up to seven passengers and reaching altitudes of 43,000 ft, with a range of 2,865 km. A distinctive feature is its over-the-wing engine mount design. The jet also offers an optional emergency auto-land system and has a list price of approximately $6.95 million.

- An emergency auto-land option is available.
