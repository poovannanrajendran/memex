## Summary
A new development from Desert Ant Labs allows AI models to run entirely on a user's device without an internet connection or subscription. These small, single-task models are free and can be integrated into applications, offering enhanced privacy and efficiency. Examples include photo organisation, data redaction, video editing, and automatic transcription, significantly reducing costs and processing times compared to cloud-based solutions.

## Key claims
- AI models can run on personal devices without an internet connection.
- Desert Ant Labs has released 18 small, free AI models for offline use.
- These models are trained for single tasks and can be integrated into apps.
- Offline AI models offer benefits such as enhanced privacy and reduced costs.
- Examples include face recognition, data redaction, video editing, and speech-to-text.

## Concepts covered
- [[offline_ai]] — Enables AI functionalities on personal devices like phones and computers, reducing reliance on cloud services, lowering costs, and improving response times, which is crucial for applications requiring high privacy and constant availability.
- [[on_device_ai]] — Crucial for privacy-sensitive applications and for providing AI features in environments with limited or no internet connectivity. It improves user experience through faster responses and can reduce operational costs for developers by offloading computation from servers.
- [[data_redaction]] — Enhances data privacy and security, especially when sensitive information needs to be sent to external services (like cloud AIs) or shared. It helps comply with privacy regulations by minimising the exposure of personal or confidential data.
