## Summary
Desert Ant Labs has released 18 small, free AI models that can run locally on a phone without an internet connection. These models are trained for specific tasks such as facial recognition, photo organisation, data redaction, video editing, and speech-to-text transcription. The article highlights how these offline models offer privacy benefits and cost savings, as demonstrated by the app Detail, which replaced its cloud AI with these local models.

## Key claims
- AI models can operate effectively on a device without requiring an internet connection.
- Desert Ant Labs has developed 18 distinct, small AI models that are free to use.
- These offline AI models offer enhanced privacy by keeping data local.
- Using local AI models can significantly reduce operational costs for applications.
- The 'Detail' iPad app has successfully integrated these offline AI models for video editing, resulting in faster processing and substantial cost savings.
