## Summary
This report from Forrester, sponsored by NiCE, addresses the significant gap between AI agent-generated insights and actual business action, with only 15% of enterprises seeing measurable ROI. It defines the 'agentic action gap' and provides a roadmap for organisations to bridge this divide. The report outlines strategies to reduce friction, accelerate action, implement orchestration, and adopt an agent-centric operating model where AI agents handle execution under human supervision.

## Key claims
- Only 15% of enterprises are achieving measurable ROI from AI agent deployments.
- The majority of AI agent initiatives stall, generating insights that do not translate into business action.
- There is a structural gap between AI agent-generated insight and value-driving business action, termed the 'agentic action gap'.
- Organisations can close this gap by measuring friction, reducing time to action, introducing orchestration, and transitioning to an agent-centric operating model.
- An agent-centric model involves AI agents executing tasks while humans provide oversight.
