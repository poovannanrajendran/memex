## Summary
An Anthropic executive predicts a hectic 18 months for cybersecurity professionals, not solely due to AI, but because AI is exposing pre-existing vulnerabilities. Many applications were built without proper security, and the rush to market meant security was an afterthought. AI is now highlighting these flaws, making patching and remediation difficult due to a lack of knowledge about running applications and resistance from departments focused on revenue. The speaker urges a move from theoretical BCPs to practical, fundamental security measures.

## Key claims
- The next 18 months will be very hectic for cybersecurity professionals.
- AI is exacerbating existing cybersecurity problems rather than creating entirely new ones.
- Many applications have fundamental security flaws that were ignored in the rush to production.
- Patching is the easy part; the real challenge is coordinating with stakeholders and implementing changes.
- Organisations often present unrealistic security and business continuity plans on slides compared to reality.
- AI's ability to find vulnerabilities is accelerating the exposure of these weaknesses.
