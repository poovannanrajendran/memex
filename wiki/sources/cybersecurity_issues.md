## Summary
The cybersecurity landscape is set to become increasingly hectic over the next 18 months, not solely due to AI, but because AI is exposing pre-existing vulnerabilities in applications and systems. Many organisations have been under the impression that their systems are secure, presenting a polished facade, but AI is rapidly revealing flaws such as unknown PHP applications or unmanaged servers. The real challenge lies not in the technical patching but in the communication and coordination across departments, like marketing and sales, who may resist halting operations for security fixes. This period requires a fundamental re-evaluation of security practices, moving beyond theoretical plans to address the tangible reality of system weaknesses.

## Key claims
- The next 18 months will be very hectic for cybersecurity professionals.
- AI is exposing existing, often hidden, cybersecurity vulnerabilities.
- Many organisations have presented a false sense of security, which AI is now shattering.
- The difficulty in addressing cybersecurity issues lies more in organisational coordination and communication than in technical solutions.
- A fundamental, real-world approach to security is needed, moving beyond presentation slides and theoretical plans.
