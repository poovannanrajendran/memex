## Summary
This YouTube video from the Tamil Guru channel, presented by Naveen Bharathi, explains the origin and meaning behind the universal power symbol found on electronic devices. It delves into the etymology of the symbol, tracing its roots to binary code and its adoption as a standard for on/off functions.

## Key claims
- The power symbol is derived from the binary digits 1 (on) and 0 (off).
- The symbol represents a combination of the 'on' and 'off' states, indicating a power toggle.
- Its widespread adoption is due to its simplicity and clear representation of a device's power status.
