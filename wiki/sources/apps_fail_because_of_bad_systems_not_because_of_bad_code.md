## Summary
This video argues that application failure is primarily due to systemic issues rather than code quality. Key systemic failures include lack of user tracking to understand behaviour, absence of a clear 'aha' moment for users to grasp value, no feedback loop to gather user input on confusion, and a lack of a consistent distribution strategy for acquiring new users. Addressing these systemic gaps is crucial for app success.

## Key claims
- Apps fail due to systemic problems, not bad code.
- Lack of user tracking prevents understanding user behaviour and drop-off points.
- Identifying and ensuring users reach an 'aha' moment quickly is critical for engagement.
- A feedback loop is necessary to understand why users leave or encounter issues.
- Consistent user acquisition and distribution are vital for app survival and improvement.
