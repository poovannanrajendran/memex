## Summary
This video introduces Jev, a new AI model developed by Tidesafe, a company founded by former OpenAI researcher Diego Almeida. Unlike traditional LLMs designed for text generation, Jev is specifically engineered for rapid decision-making in software applications. It claims to be significantly faster and cheaper than LLMs for tasks requiring thousands of real-time decisions, such as fraud detection or ticket routing.

## Key claims
- AI agents face a speed problem, not an intelligence problem.
- LLMs are too slow for making the thousands of decisions required by software in real-time.
- Jev is a new AI model from Tidesafe, founded by Diego Almeida (formerly of OpenAI).
- Jev is designed for decision-making, not text generation.
- Jev is 200 times faster and 400 times cheaper than LLMs for decision-making tasks.
- Jev's architecture is unexpected and differs from LLMs.
