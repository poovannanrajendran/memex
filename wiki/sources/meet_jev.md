## Summary
This video introduces JEV, a new AI model developed by Tidesafe, a company founded by former OpenAI researcher Dieago Almeida. Unlike traditional LLMs designed for text generation, JEV is optimised for rapid decision-making, addressing a critical speed limitation in current AI agents. It is significantly faster and cheaper than LLMs for tasks requiring thousands of decisions, such as fraud detection or ticket routing.

## Key claims
- AI agents currently face a speed problem, not an intelligence problem.
- LLMs are too slow for applications requiring rapid, numerous decisions.
- JEV is a new AI model specifically designed for fast decision-making.
- JEV is 200 times faster and 400 times cheaper than LLMs for decision-making tasks.
- JEV's architecture is unexpected and differs significantly from LLMs.
- Tidesafe is a new company founded by Dieago Almeida, a former OpenAI researcher instrumental in GPT's foundation.
