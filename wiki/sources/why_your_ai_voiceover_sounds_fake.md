## Summary
This video explains that AI voiceovers sound fake not because of the voice itself, but due to a mismatch between the voice's studio-quality and the visual content. It proposes a three-step fix: using the V3 model in ElevenLabs with a creative setting for slight wobble, applying a low-quality phone filter and a small room effect, and writing scripts with short, present-tense sentences that describe single actions. The video also stresses the importance of only cloning voices one has the right to use.

## Key claims
- AI voiceovers sound fake because of a mismatch between the voice quality and video quality.
- A "too clean" voice paired with less polished video creates an uncanny effect.
- Using ElevenLabs V3 model with a creative setting adds natural wobble to the voice.
- Applying a low-quality phone filter and a small room effect enhances realism.
- Scripts should be written with short, action-oriented sentences for better AI video generation.
