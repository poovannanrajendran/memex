## Summary
This YouTube video introduces Rufflow, an open-source AI agent system designed to overcome the limitations of single-prompt AI interactions. It highlights Rufflow's ability to deploy an 'army' of 60 AI agents working in parallel, significantly reducing costs (75% cheaper) and increasing power (2.5x) compared to standard subscriptions.
