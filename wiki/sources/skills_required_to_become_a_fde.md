## Summary
This video outlines four essential skills for aspiring Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) looking to transition from traditional software engineering. It emphasises that FDEs embed with enterprises to integrate frontier models into complex production systems. The key skills covered are protocol integration, evaluation engineering, enterprise security, and high-velocity prototyping, contrasting FDEs' role in solving the enterprise 'last-mile problem' with traditional software engineering.

## Key claims
- FDEs integrate frontier models into complex production systems within enterprises.
- Mastery of protocol integration, including standards like Model Context Protocol and structured tool calling, is crucial for secure model connection.
- Evaluation engineering, involving automated CI/CD pipelines for measuring metrics like grounding and latency, is vital.
- Enterprise security knowledge, including private cloud deployment, access control, and data residency, is a necessary skill.
- High-velocity prototyping enables rapid translation of business problems into production-ready architectures.
- FDEs bridge the gap between AI demos and scalable, compliant, integrated production systems.
