## Summary
This YouTube video ranks various job portals based on their effectiveness in helping users land job offers. It criticises LinkedIn for its crowded applicant pool and ATS issues, and ZipRecruiter for irrelevant spam. Welcome to the Jungle (formerly Otta) and Himalayas are highlighted for their user-focused approach and remote job filtering, respectively. Y Combinator jobs is praised for startup roles, while Glassdoor is noted for salary insights but not job acquisition. Hiring Cafe is ranked number one for its direct company career page aggregation and application process.

## Key claims
- LinkedIn is ineffective for job hunting due to a crowded applicant pool and Applicant Tracking System (ATS) issues.
- ZipRecruiter is characterised by spam and irrelevant job offers.
- Welcome to the Jungle (formerly Otta) is a superior alternative to LinkedIn, designed to help users get hired.
- Glassdoor is useful for salary research and company culture insights but not for securing job offers.
- Y Combinator jobs is an underrated platform for finding startup positions.
- Himalayas is an excellent resource for remote job searches, offering specific location filters.
- Hiring Cafe is the most powerful job search engine, aggregating listings directly from company career pages.
- The creator offers a comprehensive job hunting guide and a specific job portal list upon request.
