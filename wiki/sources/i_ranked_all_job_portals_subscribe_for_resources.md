## Summary
This YouTube video ranks various job portals based on their effectiveness in helping users land job offers. The creator provides a ranking from six to one, critiquing platforms like LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, and Glassdoor for issues such as excessive competition and irrelevant listings. Higher-ranked platforms like Otta (rebranded as Welcome to the Jungle), Y Combinator jobs, Himalayas, and Hiring Cafe are highlighted for their specific strengths in startup roles, remote work, and direct employer listings.

## Key claims
- LinkedIn is ranked poorly due to high competition and an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) black hole.
- ZipRecruiter is criticised for spam and irrelevant calls, making it unsuitable for high-growth careers.
- Welcome to the Jungle (formerly Otta) is presented as a better alternative to LinkedIn for job seekers.
- Glassdoor is useful for salary and company culture research but not for securing job offers.
- Y Combinator jobs is an underrated platform for startup roles, allowing direct founder contact.
- Himalayas is excellent for remote job seekers, offering filters to avoid location-specific remote roles.
- Hiring Cafe is ranked as the most powerful job search engine, aggregating listings directly from company career pages.
- Viewers are encouraged to comment 'job' to receive the full job portal list and a job hunting guide.
