## Summary
UI-TARS is an AI agent developed by ByteDance, the creators of TikTok. It operates locally on your computer, meaning your data remains private. The tool can automate tasks by interacting with your screen, moving the mouse, clicking, and filling forms as if a person were operating the machine. It offers built-in computer and browser control modes, requires no coding, and is available for both Mac and Windows.

## Key claims
- UI-TARS is a highly underrated AI tool for 2026.
- It is developed by ByteDance, the company behind TikTok.
- UI-TARS can perform tasks by directly interacting with the user's screen, including mouse movements and clicks.
- It can automate a wide range of tasks, such as booking flights and changing settings.
- The AI agent runs locally on the user's machine, ensuring data privacy.
- It includes free, built-in modes for computer and browser control.
- No coding knowledge is required to use UI-TARS.
- The tool is compatible with both Mac and Windows operating systems.
