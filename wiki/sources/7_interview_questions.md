## Summary
This YouTube short presents seven common interview questions that job candidates should be prepared to answer. The questions cover self-introduction, strengths, future aspirations, unique selling points, handling pressure, company knowledge, and candidate inquiries. It serves as a concise guide for job seekers looking to navigate the interview process effectively.

## Key claims
- There are seven essential questions commonly asked in job interviews.
- Candidates should prepare answers for questions about themselves, strengths, and future goals.
- It is important to articulate why one is the best fit for the role and how they handle pressure.
- Demonstrating knowledge of the company and asking thoughtful questions are crucial parts of an interview.
