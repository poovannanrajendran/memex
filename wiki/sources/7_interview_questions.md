## Summary
This YouTube video presents seven common interview questions that job applicants should be prepared to answer. The questions cover self-introduction, strengths, future aspirations, suitability for the role, handling pressure, company knowledge, and opportunities for the candidate to ask questions.

## Key claims
- Applicants should be ready to answer questions about themselves, their strengths, and their 5-year career goals.
- It's important to articulate why you are the best candidate and how you perform under pressure.
- Demonstrating knowledge about the hiring company is crucial.
- Always prepare questions to ask the interviewer.
