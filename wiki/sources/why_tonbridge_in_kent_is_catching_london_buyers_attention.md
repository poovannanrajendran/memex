## Summary
This video explores why Tonbridge, Kent, is becoming an attractive alternative for London property buyers. It highlights the town's historical significance, its excellent transport links to London, and its reputation as an educational hub due to its highly-rated grammar and independent schools. The video contrasts Tonbridge with its more expensive neighbours, Sevenoaks and Royal Tunbridge Wells, positioning Tonbridge as a "sweet spot" offering a strong lifestyle and educational offering at a more competitive price point.

## Key claims
- Tonbridge offers one of the most strategic commutes from Kent to London, with fast trains taking 35-45 minutes to central London stations.
- The town is a significant educational centre, boasting super-selective grammar schools and the historic Tonbridge School, attracting families seeking quality education.
- Tonbridge provides a better value proposition compared to its neighbours, Sevenoaks and Tunbridge Wells, offering similar or better amenities and connectivity at a lower price point.
- Despite recent improvements, Tonbridge retains its 'grit' and an unpretentious, active atmosphere, centred around the River Medway and its historic castle.
- Tonbridge is becoming a desirable location for buyers looking for a balance of good transport links, excellent schools, and a more affordable property market than other commuter towns in Kent.
