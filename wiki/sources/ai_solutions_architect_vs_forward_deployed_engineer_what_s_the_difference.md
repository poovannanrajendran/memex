## Summary
This video distinguishes between the roles of an AI Solutions Architect and a Forward Deployed Engineer. The former focuses on designing how AI systems integrate into a company's existing technology infrastructure, emphasizing skills like AWS, Azure, security, and system design. The latter concentrates on implementing AI solutions directly within a customer's environment, requiring expertise in Python, SQL, APIs, and LLM deployment. Both roles aim to ensure reliable, secure, and scalable AI systems, with the Forward Deployed Engineer translating technical plans into functional customer solutions.

## Key claims
- An AI Solutions Architect designs the integration of AI systems into a company's technology.
- A Forward Deployed Engineer implements AI solutions in customer environments.
- AI Solutions Architects require skills in cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), security, and system design.
- Forward Deployed Engineers need proficiency in programming (Python, SQL), APIs, and LLMs.
- Both roles are crucial for making AI systems reliable, secure, and scalable.
- Forward Deployed Engineers focus on turning technical plans into working solutions.
