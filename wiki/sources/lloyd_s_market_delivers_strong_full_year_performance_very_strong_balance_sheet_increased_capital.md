## Summary
Lloyd's announced strong full-year results for 2025, with profits rising to £10.6bn and gross written premium reaching £57.9bn. The market also saw a combined ratio of 87.6% and an increased total capital of £49.8bn. Alongside these results, Lloyd's launched a new strategy aimed at enhancing its financial edge and capital advantage.

## Key claims
- The Lloyd's market achieved a profit of £10.6bn in 2025, a 10.1% increase from the previous year.
- Gross written premium for 2025 was £57.9bn, up 4.2% from 2024.
- The combined ratio for 2025 was 87.6%, a slight increase of 0.7 percentage points.
- Total capital and reserves increased to £49.8bn, with the central solvency ratio rising to 496%.
- Lloyd's is launching a new strategy to improve underwriting performance, efficiency, and capital utilisation.
